Kingdom Resourcing

“Thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth.” — Deuteronomy 8:18

Wealth is not the goal — it is fuel. Kingdom resourcing is about generating and directing resources toward the mission: providing for your family, funding good work, and giving open-handedly.

Tentmaking and streams

Like Paul making tents, faithful men often build income streams that fund the calling. We help you think through diligent work, ethical multiple streams, and the discipline to keep the mission — not the money — at the center.

Give first, give freely

The point of getting is giving. “It is he that giveth thee power to get wealth” — so we hold it loosely and deploy it for good.
