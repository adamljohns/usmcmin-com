About

Christ-Centered Commerce

Mission: Direct household and operator commerce under Christ in the Fredericksburg region — with ownership clarity, public-brand fidelity, and the same scorecard discipline we already apply to churches and magistrates.

We publish a scored map of local businesses so Christian families can answer simple questions before they spend:

  • Who owns this?
  • How do they operate in public?
  • Would we send a brother here?
  • How do we network with them if they’re Green?

This is not “Best of the Berg.” Popularity is not holiness, and star ratings are easy to buy.

Bands

🟢 GreenRecommended for Christ-centered patronage and network
🟡 YellowCaution — usable with eyes open
🔴 RedNot recommended — material alignment or integrity failure (sourced)
⬛ BlackAvoid / alert — fraud, predation, or severe public-safety pattern
⚪ GrayUnrated — not enough evidence yet

Red is public — not a mob

“Not recommended” means do not spend here if you care about these criteria — with evidence links — not “go harass them.” Feedback is welcome. Suggest a business, correct a fact, note a connection, or appeal a rating.

Parent brand: C5iSR. Infrastructure: USMC Ministries web stack (Phase 1). Commercial invoices hit C5iSR LLC, not ministry books.
