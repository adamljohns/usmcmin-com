Color profile (v0.4)

Overall band is a judgment of the ten factor colors — not a 0–100 number. A staple with 6 Green / 3 Yellow / 1 Gray can still be overall Yellow if network-worthiness isn't earned.

🟢 GreenAffirmative evidence this factor passes
🟡 YellowCaution — mixed or unresolved
🔴 RedDocumented failure (sourced)
⬛ BlackDisqualifying pattern
⚪ GrayInsufficient evidence — neutral, not guilt

Red is public — not a mob
