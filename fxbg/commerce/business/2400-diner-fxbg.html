2400 Diner

Verdict

Classic roadside diner at 2400 Princess Anne St — a Fredericksburg fixture since the building opened in 1955. Greek immigrant family (Harry and Maria Yiasemides) purchased it in 1986 and have run it for 40 years. Harry passed in January 2022; Maria and three children continue operations. Made-from-scratch Greek and American comfort food, 7 tables, Mon–Fri 7am–8pm. Yelp updated July 2026 (155 reviews). No hostile signals. Yellow: multi-generational immigrant family ownership confirmed, strong community roots, no faith branding — verify before network introduction.

Who owns / operates it

Maria Yiasemides (current operator). Harry Yiasemides (d. January 2022, age 80) and Maria purchased the diner in 1986. Three adult children also involved in operations.
