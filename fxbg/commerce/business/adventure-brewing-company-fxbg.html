Verdict

Locally owned craft brewery in the Fredericksburg area, opened May 2014 by Tim Bornholtz, Stan Johnson, and Russ Patishnock — three homebrewing friends since the 1990s. Ownership confirmed on official website. Community-oriented taproom: free arcade, dog-friendly, food trucks, live music. Acquired and preserved the local Blue & Gray Brewing brand in 2016. Yelp: 71 reviews, updated April 2026. No hostile signals. Yellow: alcohol-serving taproom environment; family-safety holds yellow.

Who owns / operates it

Tim Bornholtz, Stan Johnson, and Russ Patishnock. Three co-founders who brewed together starting in 2010 and opened the brewery in May 2014. Ownership confirmed on brewery's own website.
