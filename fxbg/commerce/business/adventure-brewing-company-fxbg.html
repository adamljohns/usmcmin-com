Verdict

Locally owned craft brewery in the Fredericksburg area, founded in May 2014 by three homebrewing friends — Tim Bornholtz, Stan Johnson, and Russ Patishnock — who brewed together since the 1990s. Ownership confirmed on the brewery's own website. Community-oriented taproom: free arcade, dog-friendly, frequent food trucks and live music. In 2016 the brewery acquired and preserved the local Blue & Gray Brewing Company brand. Yelp: 71 reviews, updated April 2026. No hostile signals. Yellow: alcohol-serving taproom environment; verify before faith-community introductions.

Who owns / operates it

Adventure Brewing Company was founded in May 2014 by Tim Bornholtz, Stan Johnson, and Russ Patishnock. All three owners learned to brew independently in the 1990s and began brewing as a team in 2010 before opening the commercial brewery. Ownership is confirmed directly on the brewery's own website at adventurebrewing.com. The South location at 3300 Dill Smith Dr is closed; the active taproom is at 33 Perchwood Dr, Ste 101.
