Verdict

Who owns / operates it

Anthony L. Bennett Sr. founded the business in 1977, originally as Bennett's Funeral Home at 515 Princess Anne Street. It was renamed A.L. Bennett Funeral Home in 1978 to avoid confusion with another similarly named home. Anthony L. Bennett Jr. joined the family business in 2002, and the entity was formally incorporated as A.L. Bennett and Son Funeral Home, Inc. in 2006. The family also operates Bennett-Brooks Funeral Home, Inc. The business remains family-owned and operated, with multi-generational leadership now firmly established.
