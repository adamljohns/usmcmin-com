Verdict

Second-generation family-owned funeral home serving Fredericksburg since 1977. Founded by Anthony L. Bennett Sr. as Bennett’s Funeral Home at 515 Princess Anne St; Anthony Jr. joined in 2002. Incorporated as A.L. Bennett and Son Funeral Home, Inc. in 2006, now at 200 Butternut Dr. Services include traditional funerals, cremation, graveside, memorial, and veteran services. Also operates Bennett-Brooks Funeral Home, Inc. Facebook presence carries “family first” messaging. No explicit faith branding. Yellow: confirmed multi-generational family ownership, 47-year local track record — verify before network introduction.
