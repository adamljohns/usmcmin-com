Anita Crossfield is the sole owner and operator. She previously managed Italian Station at this location, then took full ownership and rebranded as Anita's Cafe & Dessert Bar. Crossfield has been recognized as the Fredericksburg Women's Club Woman of the Year 2026. The business is woman-owned and Albanian-owned (National Albanian Registry member).
