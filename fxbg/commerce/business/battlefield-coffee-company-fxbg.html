Battlefield Coffee Company

Spiritual Warfare Coffee Roastery & Espresso Bar · 6150 Plank Rd, Fredericksburg, VA 22407 · (540) 412-1480

Verdict

Veteran-owned, Christian-led coffee roastery and full-service espresso bar inside the Battlefield Country Store — a 12,000-square-foot marketplace founded in 2016 by Jeff Meier, a 21-year Air National Guard veteran and father of three. The coffee component was added in April 2021. The brand is explicitly faith-forward: the company markets itself as a "Spiritual Warfare Coffee Roastery & Espresso Bar," built on the mission "by the broken, for the broken," with direct scripture grounding ("All through scripture, God chose broken people to do the most extraordinary things"). Amish and Mennonite-made goods from partner suppliers are sold alongside the deli, bakery, bulk foods, and espresso bar. Rated 4.4/5 on TripAdvisor (66 reviews, ranked #39 of 399 restaurants in Fredericksburg as of July 2026); Yelp updated June 2026. No hostile signals of any kind.

Who owns / operates it

Jeff Meier is the publicly named founder and owner, profiled extensively in Go Fredericksburg and Fredericksburg.com features. He served 21 years in the Air National Guard and is a father of three. Meier has spoken openly about his personal story — losing his father before age six, surviving a family home fire, and experiencing homelessness — as the foundation for the business's "by the broken, for the broken" mission. His faith is the stated engine of the business, not a background detail. Owner identity is fully confirmed across public media and directory sources.
