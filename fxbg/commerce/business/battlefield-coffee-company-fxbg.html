Battlefield Coffee Company

coffee-shop · 6150 Plank Rd · Fredericksburg · VA · 22407 · (540) 412-1480
8 Green · 1 Yellow · 1 Gray

Verdict

Veteran-owned, Christian-led coffee roastery and espresso bar inside the Battlefield Country Store in Fredericksburg, VA. Founded by Jeff Meier, a 21-year Air National Guard veteran and father of three who opened the 12,000-square-foot country store marketplace in 2016 and added the coffee component in April 2021. The brand is explicitly faith-forward — named 'Spiritual Warfare Coffee Roastery & Espresso Bar' with the tagline 'by the broken, for the broken,' drawing directly on scripture ('God chose broken people to do the most extraordinary things'). The store features a full-service espresso bar, deli, bakery, bulk foods, and Amish/Mennonite-made products from partner suppliers. Rated 4.4/5 on TripAdvisor (66 reviews, ranked #39 of 399 restaurants in Fredericksburg as of July 2026); Yelp updated June 2026.

Who owns / operates it

Jeff Meier (founder; 21-year Air National Guard veteran; father of three; Battlefield Country Store opened 2016; coffee shop added April 2021; Christian-led mission)

Network path

Jeff Meier is a publicly named, profiled veteran with an explicit Christ-centered public brand — one of the strongest natural fits in the FXBG scorecard. High-confidence referral for brothers needing a coffee shop that openly shares the faith. Amish/Mennonite supplier partnerships extend the community reach beyond the store. Ideal venue for informal C5iSR meetings or bringing a brother into a faith-adjacent commercial setting.
