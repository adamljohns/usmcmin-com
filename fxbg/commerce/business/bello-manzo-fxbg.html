Bello Manzo

italian-restaurant · 715 Caroline St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 681-1566
5 Green · 3 Yellow · 2 Gray

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg Italian restaurant opened in July 2023 by husband-and-wife team Ruth and Jose Matus, who bring over 25 years of combined restaurant industry experience. The name Bello Manzo translates to 'good beef' in Italian, reflecting the menu's tribute to the quality of American beef alongside Italian flavors and Southern hospitality. Located at 715 Caroline Street in the historic district. Yelp updated April 2026 (46 reviews); Wheree updated June 2026. No faith brand signals; no hostile signals. Family-owned, owner-present operation.

Who owns / operates it

Ruth and Jose Matus (co-owners; husband-wife team; 25-plus years combined restaurant industry experience; opened July 2023)

Network path

Ruth and Jose Matus are named, publicly profiled owners of a well-regarded downtown dining option. Solid contextual referral for brothers needing a quality Italian dinner or a downtown meeting venue. No faith alignment to elevate this to a core referral, but the family-owned, husband-wife model and clean public brand make it a reliable dining recommendation.
