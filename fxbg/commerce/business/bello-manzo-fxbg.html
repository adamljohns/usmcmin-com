Bello Manzo

Italian Restaurant · 715 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 681-1566

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg Italian restaurant opened in July 2023 by husband-and-wife co-owners Ruth and Jose Matus, who bring over 25 years of combined restaurant industry experience. The name Bello Manzo — Italian for "good beef" — reflects the menu's homage to the quality of American beef served with Italian flavors and Southern hospitality. Located at 715 Caroline Street in the historic district, with a menu designed with the help of a Chicago-based executive chef. Yelp updated April 2026 (46 reviews); Wheree updated June 2026. Reviewers note being personally greeted by the owner on arrival. No faith brand signals; no hostile signals of any kind. Family-owned, owner-present operation.

Who owns / operates it

Ruth and Jose Matus are publicly named as the co-owners in a Potomac Local profile from September 2024, with their backgrounds in the restaurant industry documented. They opened Bello Manzo in July 2023. The operation is described as a husband-and-wife team with hands-on owner presence. Faith affiliation is not stated publicly.
