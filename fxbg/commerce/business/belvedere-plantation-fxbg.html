Belvedere Plantation

Farm Agritourism · Fredericksburg
7 Green · 3 Gray

Visit & contact

Address: 1410 Belvedere Dr, Fredericksburg, VA 22408

Phone: (540) 373-4478

Website →CallDirections

Verdict

645-acre heritage farm owned by the Fulks family since 1968 on the Rappahannock River, Spotsylvania County. Working production farm (grain and pumpkins). Annual Fall Harvest Festival since 1983 — 40th festival in 2023. Family-safe activities: pumpkin patch, Great Adventure Maize Maze, hayrides, bonfires, slides. Winner of Fredericksburg Parent & Family 2023 Family Favorites Seasonal Activity. 57 Yelp reviews, listing active July 2026. No faith signal; no hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Donnie and Donna Fulks, family owners since 1968. Son Ian Fulks active in operations.

Network path

Prime family outing and client hospitality path. Donnie and Donna Fulks are confirmed multi-generational owners with 50+ years of continuous community investment. Excellent brother-intro for family fall activities or out-of-town guests.
