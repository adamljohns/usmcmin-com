Belvedere Plantation

Family Farm · Agritourism · 1410 Belvedere Dr, Fredericksburg, VA 22408 · (540) 373-4478

Verdict

645-acre heritage farm owned and operated by the Fulks family since 1968 on the Rappahannock River in Spotsylvania County. A working production farm (grain and pumpkins) that has hosted the annual Fall Harvest Festival since 1983 — 40th festival in 2023. Family-safe activities include pumpkin patch, Great Adventure Maize Maze, hayrides, bonfires, and slides. Winner of Fredericksburg Parent & Family 2023 Family Favorites Seasonal Activity. 57 Yelp reviews, listing active July 2026. No faith signal, no hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Donnie and Donna Fulks, family owners since 1968. Son Ian Fulks is active in operations and the Fall Harvest Festival. Multi-generational family farm with 50+ years of continuous local presence. Ownership confirmed across Yelp, Virginia Tourism, Fredericksburg Parent & Family, and Spotsylvania County public calendar listings.
