Verdict

Large-scale commercial tulip grower at 2259 Kings Hwy in King George County, operating one of the largest cut flower greenhouse facilities on the US East Coast. CEO Werner Jansen, raised in the Netherlands, built an international supply chain spanning the US, Chile, and South Africa. In 2020, Bloomia became the first floral company to join the Fair Food Program, which certifies ethical wages and working conditions across its entire supply chain. In 2026, Bloomia opened a public Tulip Festival on its King George property, drawing regional visitors. Fredericksburg Regional Chamber member. Faith unconfirmed.
