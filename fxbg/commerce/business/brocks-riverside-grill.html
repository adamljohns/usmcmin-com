Brock's Riverside Grill

Restaurant & Event Venue · 503 Sophia St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 370-1820

Verdict

25-year Old Town Fredericksburg riverside restaurant on the Rappahannock River. Owners Jason Smith and Billy LaPointe publicly named and documented via restaurant history and news sources; both joined at founding in 1999 and became partners ~2002. Steaks, seafood, and American fare; hosts Wednesday trivia nights with 50/50 raffles benefiting Greater Fredericksburg organizations. Neutral secular brand — no faith signals, no hostile signals found. Family safety Yellow: open until 1:30 AM Thu–Sat, indicating a significant bar element on late nights. Named local owners and 25 years of reliable operation are the primary strengths.

Who owns / operates it

Jason Smith and Billy LaPointe — current owners and operators. Restaurant was founded September 1999 by Leo LaPointe and Todd Crompton; Jason Smith joined shortly after and became a partner with Billy LaPointe after ~2002. Jason's culinary background includes The Landing Restaurant on Smith Mountain Lake and Mary Washington College; Billy built his career in grocery operations (Shoppers Food Warehouse general manager).
