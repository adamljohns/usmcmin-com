Verdict

Veteran-owned acupuncture and Chinese medicine practice in Fredericksburg, founded and operated by Dr. Eddie Vernon, DACM, L.Ac. — a former U.S. Naval officer who spent seven years in uniform before earning his doctorate in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Prior to opening in Fredericksburg, Dr. Vernon served on the integrative pain team at Tripler Army Medical Center in Honolulu, providing acupuncture to active-duty service members and veterans. Services include acupuncture, Chinese herbal medicine, sound healing, breathwork, Qi Gong, and nutritional therapy. Yelp updated April 2026; AcuDirect and Chamber of Commerce profiles active. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding, but deeply veteran-mission-aligned practice with a clear mind/body/spiritual wellness ethos. Official site prints: Licensed Acupuncturist (L.Ac.).

Who owns / operates it

Dr. Edward “Eddie” Vernon, DACM, L.Ac., Dipl. Ac. Owner and sole practitioner. U.S. Navy veteran (7 years as a Naval officer). Earned a Doctor of Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine (DACM). Formerly provided acupuncture at Tripler Army Medical Center’s Interdisciplinary Pain Management Center in Honolulu, HI, treating active-duty service members and veterans alongside MDs, chiropractors, massage therapists, and yoga instructors.
