Verdict

Veteran-owned acupuncture and Chinese medicine practice in Fredericksburg, founded and operated by Dr. Eddie Vernon, DACM, L.Ac. — a former U.S. Naval officer who spent seven years in uniform before earning his doctorate in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Prior to opening in Fredericksburg, Dr. Vernon served on the integrative pain team at Tripler Army Medical Center in Honolulu, providing acupuncture to active-duty service members and veterans. Services include acupuncture, Chinese herbal medicine, sound healing, breathwork, Qi Gong, and nutritional therapy. Yelp updated April 2026; AcuDirect and Chamber of Commerce profiles active. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding, but deeply veteran-mission-aligned practice with a documented history of serving those who served.

Who owns / operates it

Dr. Edward "Eddie" Vernon, DACM, L.Ac., Dipl. Ac. owns and operates the institute as a solo practitioner. He served seven years as a U.S. Naval officer before pursuing holistic medicine. He spent four years earning his doctorate in Traditional Chinese Medicine, including study of acupuncture, Chinese herbal medicine, nutritional therapy, Tai Chi, and Qi Gong. Before establishing Central Virginia Wellness Institute (est. 2016), he worked at Tripler Army Medical Center's Interdisciplinary Pain Management Center in Honolulu, where he treated active-duty military personnel and veterans alongside a team of MDs, chiropractors, massage therapists, and yoga instructors. His integrated approach combines ancient healing disciplines with modern medical science.
