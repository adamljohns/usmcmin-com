Verdict

Brian Lam's boutique day spa at 804 Charles St in downtown Fredericksburg, founded in 2010 as Skin+Touch Therapy and rebranded Collage Spa after expanding into a historic Gothic Revival building. Chamber member; Brian Lam recognized in a 2024 Chamber entrepreneur feature. Named in a 2024 Fredericksburg Chamber entrepreneur feature. A second location serves Culpeper, Virginia.
