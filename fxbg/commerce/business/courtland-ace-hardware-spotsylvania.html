Who owns / operates it

Elizabeth McDade owns and operates Courtland Ace Hardware as a locally-owned member of the Ace Hardware cooperative. Unlike corporate-franchise models, Ace Hardware members are independent retailers in an owner-operated cooperative, giving the local owner full business autonomy. McDade has expanded the store's offerings by becoming an authorized Southern States retailer, serving the area's farming and agricultural community alongside traditional hardware customers.
