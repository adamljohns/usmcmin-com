Verdict

Locally owned real estate brokerage and property management firm at 1340 Central Park Blvd in Fredericksburg. Founded in 2004 by Michelle Caldwell Thompson, who has held a Virginia real estate license since 1992. CTI operates three divisions: real estate sales, property management, and relocation services, serving Fredericksburg, Richmond, Manassas, and Northern Virginia. With nearly 70 agents, CTI is the largest African American woman-owned real estate firm in the Fredericksburg region. Thompson was named 2025 Chair of the Fredericksburg Regional Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors. Known for homebuyer seminars and workforce development programming. Faith unconfirmed.
