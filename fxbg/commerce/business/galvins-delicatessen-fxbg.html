Galvin's Delicatessen

Deli & Speakeasy · 216 William St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401

Verdict

NYC-inspired delicatessen opened April 2025 in historic downtown Fredericksburg. Co-owners Emma and Paul Stoddard publicly named; Emma's culinary heritage traces to her great-grandfather Martin Galvin's speakeasy-era cafe in Jersey City. From-scratch operation: corned beef and pastrami brined 5+ days, all soups, salads, and bagels made in-house. Upstairs speakeasy "River's End" operates Thu–Sat. Neutral heritage-deli brand — no activist signals. Family safety Yellow due to speakeasy component. Reliability Yellow as a newer business (opened 2025). Confirm personal faith stance before network intro.

Who owns / operates it

Emma Stoddard (extensive baking and restaurant management background; now also works as a Realtor) and Paul Stoddard (long-time owner of Sunken Well Tavern since 2006), husband and wife. Named in Fredericksburg Free Press feature (June 2025) and FXBG Neighbors Podcast (Ep. 22). Registered as Galvin's Deli LLC in Virginia.
