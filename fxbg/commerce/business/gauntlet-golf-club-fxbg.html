Verdict

Public 18-hole, Par 72 golf course in Stafford County (mailing address Fredericksburg) under new local ownership since 2026. Mike Stonehill Sr. — a local home builder who also owns Hour Homes and Brookstone Homes — purchased the Gauntlet off Hartwood Road with plans to improve the clubhouse, patio, dining, golf carts, and course while keeping green fees reasonable. PGA-listed, Golf Digest profiled, and Yelp updated May 2026. Yelp shows 27 reviews active. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding; new ownership is committed to community stewardship of a public golf asset.

Who owns / operates it

Michael J. “Mike” Stonehill Sr., owner as of 2026. Also owner of Hour Homes and Brookstone Homes, local area home builders. Born and raised in the Fredericksburg region. Purchased the public course off Hartwood Road with stated plans to invest in improvements while keeping rates accessible to the community.
