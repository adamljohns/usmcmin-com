Who owns / operates it

Michael J. "Mike" Stonehill Sr. purchased the Gauntlet Golf Club in 2026. He is a longtime Fredericksburg-area home builder — owner of Hour Homes and Brookstone Homes — and a local figure in the regional construction and development community. Stonehill acquired the public course off Hartwood Road with an expressed commitment to keeping it accessible: he has stated his intent to hold green fees to reasonable levels while funding upgrades to the clubhouse, patio, dining areas, food and beverage offerings, golf carts, driving range, and the course itself over the coming years. The course's PGA listing, Golf Digest profile, and Virginia.org listing are all current.
