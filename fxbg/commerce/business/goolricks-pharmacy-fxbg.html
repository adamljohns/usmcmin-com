Goolrick's Pharmacy & Soda Fountain

Historic Landmark (Renovation / Coming Soon) · 901 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · goolricksfxbg.com

Verdict

Historic Fredericksburg pharmacy and soda fountain at 901 Caroline St — originally opened in 1892, home of what was widely called the nation's oldest continuously operating soda fountain through its 2022 closure. Purchased by the Jarrell family (Jarrell Properties Inc.) in May 2022; renovation underway as of July 2026, grand opening expected. Owned by James "Jay" Jarrell III (born and raised in Fredericksburg), his son James Jarrell IV, and daughter Jewels Jarrell Stover — a generational FXBG family reinvesting in a 130-year landmark. Currently closed for renovation; not yet operational. Gray: outstanding ownership story and community investment, but insufficient evidence on current reliability — revisit upon reopening.

Who owns / operates it

Jarrell Properties Inc. (JPI) — James "Jay" Jarrell III (President), James Jarrell IV (son), and Jewels Jarrell Stover (daughter). Jay Jarrell III was born and raised in Fredericksburg; his children James IV and Jewels also grew up here. The family purchased the Goolrick's building at 901 Caroline St in May 2022 with a stated commitment to preserving the soda fountain and relaunching the landmark for another 125+ years. Renovation documentation is posted publicly by JPI.
