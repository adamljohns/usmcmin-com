Goolrick's Pharmacy & Soda Fountain

pharmacy · 901 Caroline St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 373-3411
5 Green · 5 Gray

Verdict

Historic Fredericksburg pharmacy and soda fountain at 901 Caroline St — originally opened in 1892, home of what was considered the nation’s oldest continuously operating soda fountain through its 2022 closure. Purchased by the Jarrell family (Jarrell Properties Inc.) in May 2022; renovation underway as of July 2026, grand opening expected. Owned by James ‘Jay’ Jarrell III (born and raised in Fredericksburg), his son James Jarrell IV, and daughter Jewels Jarrell Stover. Currently closed for renovation; not yet operational. Gray: outstanding ownership story and community investment, but insufficient evidence on current reliability — revisit upon reopening.

Who owns / operates it

Jarrell Properties Inc. (JPI) — James 'Jay' Jarrell III (President), James Jarrell IV, and Jewels Jarrell Stover. Multi-generational Fredericksburg family. Purchased May 2022.

Network path

Historic 130-year-old Caroline St landmark under renovation by the Jarrell family (Jay Jarrell III, James Jarrell IV, Jewels Jarrell Stover — all FXBG-rooted). Grand opening expected 2026.
