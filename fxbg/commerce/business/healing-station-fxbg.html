The Healing Station

Biblical Counseling & Mental Health · Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 371-2704

Verdict

Faith-founded community mental health counseling center providing individual, couples, and family counseling with an explicit biblical framework. Founded by Dr. Denise L. Willis, LPC, CCTP — a retired federal employee with a master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a doctorate in Christian Counseling — alongside her husband Bishop Ron D. Willis of Vision Community Church, Stafford. Serves underserved and under-resourced populations across Planning District 16, including Fredericksburg, Stafford, King George, Spotsylvania, and Caroline. Registered 501(c)(3) (Healing Station Inc., EIN 88-2320015). Fredericksburg Regional Chamber member. No hostile signals. Yellow: Christ-centered brand confirmed via public documentary evidence; two office addresses appear in public records — confirm current primary location before a ministry-level referral.

Who owns / operates it

Dr. Denise L. Willis, LPC, CCTP — founder and principal officer of Healing Station Inc. (501c3, EIN 88-2320015). Dr. Willis holds a master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling (Trinity Washington University) and a doctorate in Christian Counseling (St. Thomas Christian University) and retired from 36 years of federal service before founding the Healing Station. Co-founded with her husband Bishop Ron D. Willis, who leads Vision Community Church in Stafford, Virginia.
