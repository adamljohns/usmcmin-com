The Healing Station

Verdict

Faith-founded community mental health counseling center providing individual, couples, and family counseling with an explicit biblical framework. Founded by Dr. Denise L. Willis, LPC, CCTP — a retired federal employee with a master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a doctorate in Christian Counseling — alongside her husband Bishop Ron D. Willis of Vision Community Church, Stafford. Serves underserved and under-resourced populations across Planning District 16, including Fredericksburg, Stafford, King George, Spotsylvania, and Caroline. Member of the Fredericksburg Regional Chamber of Commerce. Registered 501(c)(3) (Healing Station Inc., EIN 88-2320015). No hostile signals. Yellow: Christ-centered brand confirmed via public documentary evidence; two office addresses appear in public records — confirm current primary location before a ministry-level referral.

Who owns / operates it

Dr. Denise L. Willis, LPC, CCTP — founder and principal officer of Healing Station Inc. (501c3). Co-founded with her husband Bishop Ron D. Willis of Vision Community Church, Stafford VA. Dr. Willis holds a master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a doctorate in Christian Counseling.
