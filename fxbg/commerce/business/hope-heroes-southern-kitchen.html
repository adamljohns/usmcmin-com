Hope Heroes Southern Kitchen

Restaurant — Fast Casual / Social Enterprise · 9677 Patriot Hwy, Fredericksburg, VA 22407 · (540) 684-6694

Verdict

Fast-casual Southern kitchen at 9677 Patriot Hwy, founded by pastor-businessman Trent Stinson and his wife Deedre' as a social enterprise to fight food insecurity. HOPE = Helping Other People Eat. The model: buy a bowl, give a bowl — 3–5% of all profits go to Hub of Hope, Inc., the Stinsons' sister nonprofit providing food to ALICE families in the greater Fredericksburg region. Menu features Southern comfort food including smoked meats, bowls, and sandwiches. Open Fri–Sat 11am–8pm, Sun 9am–3pm; catering available. 84 Yelp reviews updated July 2026. No alcohol; family-appropriate. Faith-forward ownership with explicit pastoral leadership.

Who owns / operates it

Trent and Deedre' Stinson are the founders and owners. Trent Stinson is a pastor and businessman who also co-founded Hub of Hope, Inc., the Spotsylvania-based nonprofit fighting food insecurity that receives a portion of Hope Heroes profits. The Stinsons have operated food-based community initiatives in the Fredericksburg region since 2003. The restaurant was launched as a social enterprise to create a sustainable funding stream for Hub of Hope's anti-hunger mission.
