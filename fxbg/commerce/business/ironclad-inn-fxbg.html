Ironclad Inn & Bourbon Tasting Room

hotel-inn · 1200 Princess Anne St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 371-7622
5 Green · 2 Yellow · 3 Gray

Verdict

Historic Fredericksburg B&B at 1200 Princess Anne Street, opened December 2023 by the King family (Stephen, Owen, and Kara King), founders of Ironclad Distillery Co. in Newport News. The Kings acquired the historic Kenmore Inn — a structure built in 1793 and Fredericksburg's longest-operating inn since 1932 — for $2.4 million and rebranded it as a nine-room boutique B&B with a Stable Block Bourbon Tasting Room downstairs. Yelp updated July 2026; TripAdvisor 102+ reviews. No faith brand signals; no hostile signals. Alcohol (bourbon) is a primary offering — Family safety Yellow.

Who owns / operates it

Stephen King, Owen King, and Kara King (founders of Ironclad Distillery Co., Newport News VA)

Network path

Stephen, Owen, and Kara King are publicly named owners of a well-documented boutique inn in historic downtown Fredericksburg. Solid referral for brothers hosting out-of-town guests or planning a unique local experience. The bourbon tasting room is a primary feature — appropriate for network use by context and conscience. Not a family dining anchor or a discipleship referral point, but a quality lodging recommendation any brother can pass confidently.
