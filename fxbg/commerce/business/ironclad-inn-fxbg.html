Ironclad Inn & Bourbon Tasting Room

Boutique B&B & Bourbon · 1200 Princess Anne St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 371-7622

Verdict

Historic nine-room bed-and-breakfast in downtown Fredericksburg, housed in a structure built in 1793 that has operated continuously as an inn since 1932. Purchased by the King family — Stephen, Owen, and Kara King, founders of Ironclad Distillery Co. in Newport News — for $2.4 million in January 2024 and rebranded as the Ironclad Inn & Bourbon Tasting Room. The Stable Block Bourbon Room on the ground floor is open to inn guests and the public for bourbon tastings, craft cocktails, and rotating food plates. Yelp updated July 2026; TripAdvisor 102+ reviews. Fredericksburg Chamber of Commerce member. No faith brand signals; no hostile signals. Bourbon is the primary brand identity — Family safety Yellow by default.

Who owns / operates it

Stephen King, Owen King, and Kara King are publicly named as the three owners in multiple press accounts, including coverage of the January 2024 acquisition and the April 2024 Fredericksburg Free Press profile. The Kings founded Ironclad Distillery Co. in Newport News, Virginia, in 2014, where they craft bourbon. The $2.4 million purchase of the historic Kenmore Inn property was documented in regional business press. Faith affiliation is not stated publicly.
