J. Brian's Tap Room

restaurant · 200 Hanover St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 373-0738
5 Green · 2 Yellow · 3 Gray

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg Irish pub and gastropub at 200 Hanover St, open since 1994 at the corner of Caroline and Hanover streets. Current owner Mike Mansfield purchased the business in November 2022 after 20+ years on staff. Menu: smoked pork BBQ from an in-house Texas smoker, Irish entrees, handcrafted pizzas, 24 beers on tap. Standout community fruit: Mansfield's team created the J. Brian's 15K road race, raising nearly $50,000 for Wounded Warrior Project and Team Red White & Blue – Fredericksburg. 165 Yelp reviews (updated March 2026). Bar open until 2am daily — alcohol-primary nightlife venue. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals.

Who owns / operates it

Mike Mansfield (owner since November 2022; worked at J. Brian's 20+ years as bartender, night manager, and part-owner before buying)

Network path

Mike Mansfield is a publicly named local owner with a 30-year connection to the building and strong veterans-charity track record. J. Brian's is a bar and pub — not a default family table — but the veterans fundraising and decades of community presence make it a contextual resource for brothers with service ties. Early-evening or daytime visit is the cleaner C5iSR context. Faith unconfirmed — confirm before C5iSR intro.
