J. Brian's Tap Room

Pub / Gastropub · 200 Hanover St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 373-0738

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg Irish pub and gastropub at 200 Hanover St, operating since 1994 at the corner of Caroline and Hanover streets. Current owner Mike Mansfield purchased the business in November 2022 after working there for more than 20 years as bartender, night manager, and eventually part-owner. Menu features smoked pork BBQ from an in-house Texas smoker, Irish entrees, and handcrafted pizzas, with 24 beers on tap. Standout community fruit: Mansfield's team created the J. Brian's 15K road race, raising nearly $50,000 for the Wounded Warrior Project and Team Red White & Blue – Fredericksburg. 165 Yelp reviews (updated March 2026). Bar open until 2am daily — alcohol-primary nightlife venue. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals.

Who owns / operates it

Mike Mansfield is the confirmed owner, having purchased J. Brian's from founders Jack and Karen Hyland in November 2022. Mansfield's tenure at J. Brian's spans more than 20 years — starting as a bartender, advancing to night manager, then becoming a part-owner before completing the full acquisition. He has publicly stated his intent to maintain the pub's character while making incremental improvements. The Hyland family operated the business from its 1994 founding.
