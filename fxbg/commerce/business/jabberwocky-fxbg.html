Verdict

Independent children’s bookstore and toy shop operating in Historic Downtown Fredericksburg at 810 Caroline Street since 1985. Business entity: Jabberwocky Inc. The shop carries a curated selection of children’s literature, educational toys, games, plushies, and gifts emphasizing imagination and learning. One of the longest-continuously-operating independent retailers in downtown FXBG — over 40 years in the historic corridor. Listed as a Bookshop.org independent bookshop affiliate.
