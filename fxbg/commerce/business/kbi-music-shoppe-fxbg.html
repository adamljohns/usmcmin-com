KBI Music Shoppe

Music Retail & Lessons · 4950 Southpoint Dr, Fredericksburg, VA 22407 · (540) 891-7800

Verdict

Family-owned band instrument repair, rental, and lessons shop founded in 2009 by Ricia King (JMU, Music Education) and Dan King (U.S. Marine Corps veteran, 12 years as trumpet player, Red Wing Band Instrument Repair degree). 4950 Southpoint Dr, Fredericksburg VA 22407. Started in the Kings' basement to serve local school bands; now a full storefront offering rentals, repairs, music lessons, and accessories. Carries their own FIDELIS Musical Instruments brand. Yelp 5.0 (7 reviews), updated May 2026. Facebook 100% recommend (15 reviews). No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Ricia King, owner. Dan King, owner and head repair technician. Ricia is a JMU graduate with a BA in Music Education (emphasis in Flute) and a former USAirways flight attendant. Dan is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran who played trumpet in the Corps for 12 years and holds a degree in Band Instrument Repair from Red Wing Technical College. The couple founded KBI in 2009 out of their basement to bring affordable instrument repair to local school music programs. Ownership confirmed via LinkedIn (Ricia King) and Alignable.
