Verdict

Family-owned band instrument repair, rental, and lessons shop founded in 2009 by Ricia King (JMU, Music Education) and Dan King (U.S. Marine Corps veteran, 12 years trumpet, Red Wing Band Instrument Repair degree). 4950 Southpoint Dr, Fredericksburg VA 22407. Started in the Kings' basement serving local school bands; now a full storefront. Carries their own FIDELIS Musical Instruments brand. Yelp 5.0 rating (7 reviews), updated May 2026. Facebook 100% recommend (15 reviews). Serves school students, adult learners, and local music programs. No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Ricia King, owner. Dan King, owner/repair technician. Dan is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran — 12 years playing trumpet in the Corps. Degree in Band Instrument Repair from Red Wing Technical College.
