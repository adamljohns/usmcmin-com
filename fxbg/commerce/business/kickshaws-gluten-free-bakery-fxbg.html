Verdict

Dedicated allergen-free bakery on Plank Rd in Fredericksburg, owned and operated by Kathy Craddock, who founded the business after her family — including her son and daughter — were diagnosed with celiac disease and severe food allergies. All products are certified top-9 allergen-free and 100% vegan. Won 2nd place at the National Capital Area Cake Show. In July 2026, Kathy opened a second location in Richmond's Northside (per Richmond BizSense), confirming the Fredericksburg location's continued operation as a growing business. Active website and Yelp. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith-community branding; primarily a specialty food business serving a real dietary need.

Who owns / operates it

Kickshaws Gluten-Free Bakery is owned by Kathy Craddock, who serves as founder and CEO. Kathy discovered her son had celiac disease and her daughter had a severe dairy allergy, which forced her to relearn baking entirely from scratch. She turned that necessity into a mission: provide safe, genuinely allergen-free baked goods to people who have no other options. Ownership confirmed on the business's own About page and on LinkedIn. Address: 3631 Plank Rd, Fredericksburg, VA 22407. Hours: Sun 10 AM–2 PM, Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat 9 AM–4 PM, closed Mon/Wed.
