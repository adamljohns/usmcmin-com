Verdict

Dedicated allergen-free bakery on Plank Rd in Fredericksburg, owned and operated by Kathy Craddock, who founded the business after her family was diagnosed with celiac disease and severe food allergies. All products are top-9 allergen-free and 100% vegan. Won 2nd place at the National Capital Area Cake Show. Expanding with a second location in Richmond’s Northside (July 2026 per Richmond BizSense). Consistent hours, active website and Yelp. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith-community branding; niche specialty food business.

Who owns / operates it
