Let's Break Bread

Verdict

Veteran-owned comfort food pop-up and catering business at 104 Wolfe St in downtown Fredericksburg, operated by Maria Rowena Taylor, a retired U.S. military veteran and self-taught chef. Menu rotates by day with international comfort food — Filipino dishes on Fridays, Korean on Saturdays, and a global spread on Sundays — plus cooking classes, meal prep, and event catering. Ownership confirmed directly on the business's own About page and via GoFredericksburg and the Fredericksburg Free Press. FXBG.com listed. No hostile signals. Yellow: weekend-only dining hours are intentional (pop-up model); business website domain was unresolvable at time of scoring — verify current digital access before a formal intro.

Who owns / operates it

Let's Break Bread is owned and operated by Maria Rowena Taylor, a retired U.S. military veteran, mother of three daughters, and passionate self-taught chef. Maria describes herself as a "natural, self-taught, and educated chef" who creates meals meant to be shared. The business evolved from its collaboration with Rehana's historic event venue and now operates as its own catering and pop-up restaurant at 104 Wolfe St. Entity: Let's Break Bread Home Cooking and Catering, LLC. Ownership confirmed on the business's own About page, GoFredericksburg feature, and Fredericksburg Free Press Biz Beat (January 2025).
