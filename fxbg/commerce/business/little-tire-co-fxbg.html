Who owns / operates it

F. Raymond "Ray" Little founded the company in 1959 after managing a B.F. Goodrich store in the area. His sons, Michael R. Little (President) and David V. Little (VP/Treasurer), inherited the business in the 1990s and expanded from one to three locations across the Fredericksburg area: Princess Anne St, Plank Rd, and Patriot Hwy. Three generations of the Little family are now actively involved. Ownership confirmed by Tire Review Magazine profile, Free Lance-Star feature, and Fredericksburg Chamber of Commerce Small Business Spotlight.
