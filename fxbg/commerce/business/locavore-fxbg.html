Locavore

restaurant · 314 William St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 681-1800
7 Green · 3 Gray

Verdict

Farm-to-table New American restaurant at 314 William St in Downtown Fredericksburg, opened August 2025 by Chef-Owner Daniel Perron. Perron brings nearly two decades of DC-area fine-dining experience — Michelin Bib Gourmand and Washingtonian Top 100 at Whaley's, Executive Chef at Charlie Palmer Steakhouse on Capitol Hill. Menu drives off a custom konro grill with Mid-Atlantic farm sourcing. Attached rooftop bar Folklore serves Southern bites and craft cocktails. Yelp: 33 reviews, 59 photos, updated June 2026. Tripadvisor: 4.5/5. No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Daniel Perron, chef-owner. Former Executive Chef at Charlie Palmer Steakhouse (Capitol Hill), Whaley's (Michelin Bib Gourmand), and Trummer's.

Network path

Best local table in FXBG for a special occasion or client dinner. Chef Daniel Perron is the real deal — DC fine-dining pedigree, serious farm sourcing, no pretension. Rooftop bar Folklore is a bonus. Reservations via Resy or locavoreva.com. 314 William St. (540) 681-1800 · locavoreva.com
