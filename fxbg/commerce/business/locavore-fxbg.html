Locavore

Farm-to-Table Restaurant · New American · 314 William St, Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 681-1800

Verdict

Serious farm-to-table New American restaurant in Downtown Fredericksburg, opened August 2025 by Chef-Owner Daniel Perron. Perron brings nearly two decades of DC fine-dining experience — including Michelin Bib Gourmand at Whaley's, Washingtonian Top 100, and Executive Chef at Charlie Palmer Steakhouse on Capitol Hill. Menu centers on a custom konro grill and sources from Mid-Atlantic farms. Attached rooftop bar Folklore offers Southern bites and craft cocktails. Yelp: 33 reviews, 59 photos, updated June 2026. Tripadvisor: 4.5/5. No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Daniel Perron, chef-owner. With nearly two decades at the top of the Washington DC dining scene, Perron earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand and Washingtonian Top 100 nod at Whaley's, and landed Trummer's at number four in Northern Virginia Magazine. Most recently Executive Chef at Charlie Palmer Steakhouse on Capitol Hill. His Korean heritage influences signature dishes like Kalbi beef skewers alongside the wood-fire, farm-forward cooking style. City of Fredericksburg featured Perron in a 2025/2026 city spotlight profile. Operating out of 314 William St in Old Town Fredericksburg.
