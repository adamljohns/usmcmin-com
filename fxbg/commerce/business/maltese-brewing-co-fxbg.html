Verdict

Firefighter-founded craft brewery and taproom opened 2015 in Spotsylvania County, named after the Maltese Cross on firefighters' badges. Family safety Yellow — alcohol primary venue, open until 10 PM Thu–Sat. Active community anchor with strong local identity.

Who owns / operates it

Founded in 2015 by Bobby Cook and Joseph Smith, both professional firefighters who grew their garage homebrewing hobby into a commercial operation. Named after the Maltese Cross — the emblem on many firefighters' badges. Current ownership expanded to four principals: Bobby Cook, Brian Bailey, Everett Hinson, and Bruce Bolieau. Spotsylvania County features the business on its official economic development pages.
