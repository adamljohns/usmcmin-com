Matern Staffing

Staffing & Employment Agency · 10712 Ballantraye Dr, Ste 312, Fredericksburg, VA 22407 · (540) 604-5000

Verdict

Third-generation woman-owned staffing agency serving Fredericksburg and the greater Mid-Atlantic region since 1967. Founded by Ann Duffer; led today by her granddaughter Ginni Matern (owner and president), who acquired the business (then AT Once Staffing) in 2017 and rebranded it as Matern Staffing, Inc. Services: temporary, temp-to-perm, and direct-hire placement across professional, light-industrial, and clerical roles. Named a Top Virginia Employer for Interns by SCHEV in 2025 and 2026. Ginni Matern is the 2026 Fredericksburg Regional Chamber of Commerce board chair, and serves on the Truist Advisory Board, Spotsylvania Economic Development Board, GO Virginia Region 6 Council, and the Bay Consortium Workforce Development Board. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals.

Who owns / operates it

Ginni Matern is the third-generation owner and president. Her grandmother Ann Duffer founded the company in 1967 in Richmond; Ginni purchased it in January 2017 and rebranded from AT Once Staffing to Matern Staffing, Inc. She is a graduate of Leadership Fredericksburg's 2020 class, the 2026 Fredericksburg Chamber board chair, and an active sponsor and speaker at regional business and community events.
