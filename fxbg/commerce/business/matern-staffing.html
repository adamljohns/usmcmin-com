Matern Staffing

staffing-employment · 10712 Ballantraye Dr, Ste 312 · Fredericksburg · VA · 22407 · (540) 604-5000
7 Green · 3 Gray

Verdict

Third-generation woman-owned staffing agency serving Fredericksburg and the greater Mid-Atlantic region since 1967. Founded by Ann Duffer; led today by granddaughter Ginni Matern (owner and president), who acquired the business (then AT Once Staffing) in 2017 and rebranded it as Matern Staffing, Inc. Services: temporary, temp-to-perm, and direct-hire placement across professional, light-industrial, and clerical roles. Named a Top Virginia Employer for Interns by SCHEV in 2025 and 2026. Ginni Matern is the 2026 Fredericksburg Regional Chamber of Commerce board chair, and serves on the Truist Advisory Board, Spotsylvania Economic Development Board, GO Virginia Region 6 Council, and the Bay Consortium Workforce Development Board. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals.

Who owns / operates it

Ginni Matern (third-generation owner and president; 2026 Fredericksburg Regional Chamber board chair)

Network path

Ginni Matern is the 2026 Fredericksburg Chamber board chair with a community footprint across regional economic development, workforce development, and banking advisory boards. Solid C5iSR resource for brothers who need temp or direct-hire staffing solutions, or who are employers looking for a trusted local recruiting partner. Third-generation ownership and 55+ years of regional presence are strong reliability signals. Faith unconfirmed — confirm before deeper C5iSR intro.
