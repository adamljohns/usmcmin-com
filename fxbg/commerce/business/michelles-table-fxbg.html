Michelle's Table

Personal Chef & Catering · Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 903-1447

Verdict

Locally, woman-owned personal chef and catering business founded and operated by Michelle Duggins in Fredericksburg. A former registered nurse of 13 years, Duggins built Michelle's Table from the ground up — she began posting custom menus during the COVID-19 pandemic and officially left nursing to run the business full-time. Entirely self-taught; all items are made from scratch including sauces and dressings. Serves private parties, weddings, baby and bridal showers, anniversaries, corporate meetings, and charity events with fully customized menus. Featured by the City of Fredericksburg Economic Development office and recognized in Women's History Month coverage by FXBG.com. No hostile signals found. Yellow: owner confirmed through multiple public sources including the city's own business spotlight; no faith branding in either direction — verify before network introduction.
