Nathan began learning flooring installation as a boy, helping his father, and turned that foundation into more than two decades of professional experience. The couple opened their Fredericksburg showroom and subsequently added a Builder Showroom at 210 Freedom Court to serve contractors and home builders directly. Fredericksburg Parent & Family profiled the Owens in March 2026, confirming their ownership and tracing Nathan's trade history.
