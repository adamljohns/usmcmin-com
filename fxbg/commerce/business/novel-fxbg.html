Verdict

Independent bookstore and gift shop in downtown Fredericksburg, opened April 2025 by Andie Ayers — a 20-year FXBG resident who previously founded the Fairy Godmother Project, a local nonprofit serving families facing pediatric cancer. Ayers launched Novel after a personal breast cancer diagnosis sent her toward a new chapter; the store carries curated fiction and nonfiction, unique gifts, and cards chosen for people navigating hard seasons. The shop shares space with Green Eyed Vines, a local plant nursery run by husband-and-wife team Regan and Donnie Woolford. Yelp updated June 2026; Facebook and Bookshop.org active. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding, but deeply community-rooted mission and owner with a documented record of servant leadership.

Who owns / operates it

Andie Ayers, owner and operator. Former executive director of the Fairy Godmother Project, a Fredericksburg nonprofit serving families facing pediatric cancer. Breast cancer survivor. Has lived in Fredericksburg since 2006.
