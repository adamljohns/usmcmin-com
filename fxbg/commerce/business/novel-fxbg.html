Novel

Independent Bookstore & Gift Shop · 212 William St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 200-8224

Verdict

Independent bookstore and gift shop in downtown Fredericksburg, opened April 2025 by Andie Ayers — a 20-year FXBG resident who previously founded the Fairy Godmother Project, a nonprofit serving families facing pediatric cancer. Ayers launched Novel after a personal breast cancer diagnosis sent her toward a new chapter; the store carries curated fiction and nonfiction, unique gifts, and cards chosen for people navigating hard seasons. The shop shares its space with Green Eyed Vines, a local plant nursery run by husband-and-wife team Regan and Donnie Woolford. Yelp listing updated June 2026; Facebook and Bookshop.org profiles active. No hostile signals of any kind. Yellow: no explicit faith branding, but deeply community-rooted mission and owner with a strong record of servant leadership.

Who owns / operates it

Andie Ayers owns and operates Novel. She has lived in Fredericksburg since 2006, originally from Western New York. Before Novel she founded the Fairy Godmother Project, a local nonprofit that served families facing pediatric cancer for roughly fifteen years. Around 2022, while preparing to transition out of the nonprofit, Ayers was diagnosed with breast cancer. During treatment she began selling cards and books that support people going through difficult things — experiences born from her years serving pediatric cancer families and her own journey through diagnosis. Novel grew directly from that work. The store is owner-operated, independent, and locally rooted.
