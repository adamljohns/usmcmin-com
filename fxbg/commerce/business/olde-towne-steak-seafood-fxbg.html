Verdict

Classic fine dining restaurant in downtown Fredericksburg serving steaks and seafood since 1981. Located at 1612 Caroline St. Husband-and-wife operation: Greg Harding (owner/chef) and Hyon Harding. Signature dishes include crab imperial, crab cakes, and hand-cut steaks. Elegant atmosphere with wood-paneled walls and fireplaces. Open Tuesday–Sunday 4:00–9:00 pm.
