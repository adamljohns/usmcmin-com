Orleans Bistro & Grill

Cajun & Creole Restaurant · 5442 Southpoint Plaza Way, Fredericksburg, VA 22407 · (540) 891-4440

Verdict

Cajun Creole restaurant opened in October 2017 by owner Tisha Johnson at Southpoint Plaza in Fredericksburg. Serves New Orleans-style cuisine — shrimp and grits, fried chicken, beignets — with a Southern community emphasis. Owner Tisha Johnson is a Fredericksburg Chamber member and North Stafford Rotary Club board president, recognized as 2017 Rotarian of the Year; the restaurant actively supports local nonprofits. Active and well-reviewed: 498 Yelp reviews (updated July 2026) and 1,634 Facebook recommendations (94% recommend). Evening nightlife programming — DJ Fridays at 9 p.m., weekend brunch day parties with live music — earns a Yellow on the family-safety axis. No explicit faith brand signals; no hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Tisha Johnson is the publicly named owner, featured by name in the restaurant's own YouTube commercial and consistently identified as owner across media, Rotary records, and business directories. She opened her first restaurant, Buttercups Cafe, in 2013 before launching Orleans Bistro & Grill in 2017. Her civic engagement — Rotary board president, nonprofit partnerships, Chamber membership — is documented in public sources. Faith affiliation is not stated publicly.
