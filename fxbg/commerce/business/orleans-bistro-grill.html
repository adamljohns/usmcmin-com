Orleans Bistro & Grill

cajun-restaurant · 5442 Southpoint Plaza Way · Fredericksburg · VA · 22407 · (540) 891-4440
5 Green · 3 Yellow · 2 Gray

Verdict

Cajun Creole restaurant opened in October 2017 by owner Tisha Johnson at Southpoint Plaza in Fredericksburg. Serves New Orleans-style cuisine — shrimp and grits, fried chicken, beignets — with a Southern community emphasis. Owner Tisha Johnson is a chamber member and North Stafford Rotary Club board president, recognized as 2017 Rotarian of the Year; the restaurant actively supports local nonprofits. 498 Yelp reviews (updated July 2026) and 1,634 Facebook recommendations (94% recommend). Evening nightlife programming (DJ Fridays at 9 p.m., weekend brunch day parties) earns a Yellow on the family-safety axis. No explicit faith brand signals; no hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Tisha Johnson (owner; opened October 2017; North Stafford Rotary Club board president, 2017 Rotarian of the Year; Chamber member; community-focused)

Network path

Tisha Johnson is a named, publicly engaged owner with an active community track record. Good contextual dining referral for brothers needing a local gathering space. Evening nightlife programming (DJ, line dancing, karaoke) makes this context-dependent for family or faith-group hosting. Not a discipleship-core venue; dining and community utility only.
