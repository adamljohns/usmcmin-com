Orofino Restaurant

Verdict

Danilo and Alona Orofino’s Sicilian family restaurant at 1006 Caroline St — a downtown FXBG institution since 2016. Danilo honed his craft in Palermo and at Eataly in Manhattan before settling in Fredericksburg, first running Basilico then opening his eponymous restaurant. 595 Yelp reviews updated July 2026; OpenTable reservations active. Dinner Mon–Sat; closed Sunday. Clean brand, no hostile signals. Ownership Green (named principals, 10-year FXBG track record). Community fruit Green — immigrant family anchor in the downtown dining scene.

Who owns / operates it

Danilo Orofino, owner/chef. Born Palermo, Sicily. Previously ran Basilico in Fredericksburg. Partner: Alona Orofino. Est. 2016.

Network path

Downtown Italian restaurant for dining referrals. Family-owned immigrant success story — solid intro for any brother seeking quality local dining. Not discipleship core.
