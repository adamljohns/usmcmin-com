Orofino Restaurant

Italian Restaurant · 1006 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 373-1352

Verdict

Danilo and Alona Orofino's Sicilian family restaurant at 1006 Caroline St — a downtown FXBG institution since 2016. Danilo was born in Palermo, Sicily, trained in the culinary arts there, then worked at Eataly in Manhattan where he met his wife Alona. The couple settled in Fredericksburg, where Danilo first ran the popular Basilico before launching Orofino as his namesake restaurant. 595 Yelp reviews updated July 2026; reservations via OpenTable; dinner service Mon–Sat, closed Sunday. Clean brand, no hostile signals. Ownership Green (named principals, 10-year FXBG track record). Community fruit Green — immigrant family anchor in the downtown dining scene.

Who owns / operates it

Danilo Orofino, owner and executive chef. Born in Palermo, Sicily, where he trained in culinary arts and restaurant management. Previously ran Basilico in Fredericksburg. Business partner and wife: Alona Orofino. Restaurant established 2016 at 1006 Caroline St (3rd block), downtown Fredericksburg.
