Pearlescent Candle Co.

Candles & Home Goods · 809 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 993-2287

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg candle and home goods shop at 809 Caroline St, owned and founded by Eric Campbell. Campbell left the corporate world and started with small candle pop-ups, growing the concept into a brick-and-mortar store within a year. The shop features 100+ non-toxic, hand-poured signature scents along with daily candle-making workshops and perfume/cologne experiences. A second location operates at Spotsylvania Towne Centre. Pearlescent has been recognized as a 2026 FXBG Diverse City business. Brand centers on inclusion, human connection, and welcoming atmosphere — no hostile signals detected. Faith signal not publicly confirmed.

Who owns / operates it

Eric Campbell is the publicly named founder and owner, confirmed by the Fredericksburg Economic Development Authority, FXBG Diverse City Spotlight, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and FXBG.com. Campbell left a corporate career he disliked and grew Pearlescent from a mall candle pop-up into one of downtown Fredericksburg's best-reviewed gift shops. His mother sold PartyLite when he was younger, informing his passion for the craft. He is a hands-on operator with a sustained public presence in the Fredericksburg business community.
