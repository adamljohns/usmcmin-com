Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg candle and home goods shop at 809 Caroline St, owned and founded by Eric Campbell. Campbell left the corporate world and started with small candle pop-ups, growing the concept into a brick-and-mortar store within a year. Features 100+ non-toxic, hand-poured signature scents and daily candle-making workshops. Second location at Spotsylvania Towne Centre. Recognized as a 2026 FXBG Diverse City business. Brand centers on inclusion, human connection, and welcoming atmosphere — no hostile signals detected. Faith signal not publicly confirmed.
