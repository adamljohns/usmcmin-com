Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg art gallery and working studio opened in 2010 by husband and wife Gabriel and Scarlett Pons. Features ceramics, fine art, jewelry, and handmade goods from regional artists; offers art classes in ceramics, painting, mixed media, and street art for children and adults. Gabriel is a painter and muralist (FCCA juror); Scarlett is a self-taught potter with an Architecture background. Neutral community-arts brand — no hostile signals. Faith signal not publicly stated.
