Ponshop Studio and Gallery

Art Gallery & Studio · 712 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 656-2215

Verdict

Downtown Fredericksburg art gallery and working studio opened in 2010 by husband and wife Gabriel and Scarlett Pons. Features ceramics, fine art, jewelry, and handmade goods from regional and national artists; offers art classes in ceramics, painting, mixed media, and street art for children and adults. Gabriel is a painter and muralist; Scarlett is a self-taught potter with an Architecture background. Neutral community-arts brand — no hostile signals detected. Family-friendly content and education focus. Faith signal not publicly stated; confirm personal alignment before C5iSR network intro.

Who owns / operates it

Gabriel and Scarlett Pons, husband and wife, co-founded Ponshop in 2010. Gabriel is a painter, muralist, and graphic artist (skateboard graphics, street art) who has served as a juror for the Fredericksburg Center for the Creative Arts. Scarlett holds a degree in Architecture and is a self-taught studio potter. Both are publicly named as co-founders on the business website and across multiple local listings. No legal entity name found in search results; owner names confirmed via official site, Yelp, and FCCA juror listing.
