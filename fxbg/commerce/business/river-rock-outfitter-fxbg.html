Verdict

Locally owned outdoor gear, apparel, and instruction shop established in 2014 by April and Keith Peterson; Keith is a Marine veteran. Offers guided paddling, rock climbing, hiking, and fly fishing on the Rappahannock River. Named 'Champion of the Chesapeake' (Small Business category) by the Chesapeake Conservancy for environmental stewardship and community education. SWaM-certified MicroBusiness. Downtown location at 708 Caroline St opened January 2025; second location at 1382 Carl D Silver Pkwy. Neutral conservation brand — no hostile signals. Faith signal unconfirmed.
