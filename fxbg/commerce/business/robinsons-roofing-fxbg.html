Verdict

Locally owned roofing and home services company founded in 2019 by Thomas Robinson and publicly marketed as “A Christian Owned Company.” Services include residential roofing installation and repair, gutters, siding, and windows across Fredericksburg, Stafford, Spotsylvania, and surrounding Northeastern Virginia counties. Holds a BBB A+ rating with 97 verified reviews on Birdeye. Thomas Robinson was recognized by goFredericksburg.com as a Father of the Year recipient, with values of faith, integrity, and personal accountability documented in a public profile. Offers a 10% discount to military and first responders. Fully licensed and insured.

Who owns / operates it

Thomas Robinson (owner; family-owned; founded 2019; goFredericksburg Father of the Year recipient; explicitly Christian-owned)
