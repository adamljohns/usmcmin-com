Sophia Street Studios

art-gallery · 1104 Sophia St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 372-3459
6 Green · 1 Yellow · 3 Gray

Verdict

Independent fine handcrafts studio and gallery at 1104 Sophia St in Downtown Fredericksburg, owned by Trista Chapman (potter) with partner Sara Branner (SaraGray Designs) since 2021. Offers one-of-a-kind pottery, stained glass, jewelry, clothing, metal art, and unique gifts. A back gallery now features approximately 15 local artists. Hours: Wed–Sat 10am–5pm or by appointment. Yelp updated February 2026. Listed by City of Fredericksburg Economic Development and visitfred.com. No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Trista Chapman, owner and potter. Partner Sara Branner (SaraGray Designs) since 2021. Features approximately 15 local artists in the gallery.

Network path

Best in FXBG for handcrafted gifts — pottery, stained glass, jewelry, and local art. Trista Chapman is a well-regarded downtown maker. Open Wed–Sat 10am–5pm; call ahead outside those hours. 1104 Sophia St. (540) 372-3459 · sophiastreetstudios.com
