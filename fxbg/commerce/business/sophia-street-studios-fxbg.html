Sophia Street Studios

Fine Handcrafts · Art Gallery · 1104 Sophia St, Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 372-3459

Verdict

Independent fine handcrafts studio and gallery at 1104 Sophia St in Downtown Fredericksburg, owned and operated by Trista Chapman (potter) with partner Sara Branner (SaraGray Designs) since 2021. Offers one-of-a-kind pottery, stained glass, jewelry, clothing, metal art, and unique gifts. A back gallery opened in recent years now features approximately 15 local artists. Yelp updated February 2026. Listed by City of Fredericksburg Economic Development and on visitfred.com. Hours: Wed–Sat 10am–5pm or by appointment. No hostile signals.

Who owns / operates it

Trista Chapman, owner and potter. Chapman is the founding artisan at 1104 Sophia St, known for generous community spirit — customers describe her as "the most pleasant real person" and fellow artists note she shares craft knowledge freely. Sara Branner (SaraGray Designs) joined as partner in 2021, expanding the studio's design offerings. The duo now hosts around 15 local artists in the gallery space. City of Fredericksburg Economic Development profile confirmed Trista Chapman and Sara Branner as the operating team. Confirmed address and phone via Yelp, Yellow Pages, and visitfred.com listings.
