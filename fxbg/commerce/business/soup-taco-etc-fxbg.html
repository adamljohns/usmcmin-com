Soup & Taco, Etc.

Mexican & Latin American Restaurant · 813 Caroline St, Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 899-0969

Verdict

Family-owned Latin American restaurant on Caroline Street in downtown Fredericksburg, operated by twin brothers Jose and Robert Gonzalez, whose family emigrated from El Salvador. The brothers purchased the original restaurant in 2008 during the recession and have run it for over 16 years. A second location (Soup & Taco 2) at 806 William St opened in 2016. Menu spans tacos, pupusas, tamales de elote, and soups drawn from El Salvador, Cuba, Venezuela, and Peru. Yelp: 332 reviews, updated June 2026. Virginia.org listed. No hostile signals. Yellow: owner names confirmed via local press, not on the official website's about section.

Who owns / operates it

Soup & Taco, Etc. is owned by the Gonzalez family — twin brothers Jose and Robert Gonzalez, whose parents emigrated from El Salvador and spent decades in the restaurant industry before the family entered for themselves. Jose and Robert purchased the original restaurant at 813 Caroline St in 2008 from its founding husband-and-wife team and have grown it to two downtown locations. Identity confirmed through multiple local news sources including the Fredericksburg Free Press and the City of Fredericksburg; not listed on the restaurant's own website.
