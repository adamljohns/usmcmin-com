Soup & Taco, Etc.

Verdict

Family-owned Latin American restaurant at 813 Caroline St in downtown Fredericksburg, operated by twin brothers Jose and Robert Gonzalez whose family is from El Salvador. Original restaurant purchased in 2008; second location (Soup & Taco 2) opened at 806 William St in 2016. Menu includes tacos, pupusas, tamales de elote, and soups from El Salvador, Cuba, Venezuela, and Peru. Yelp: 332 reviews, updated June 2026. Virginia.org listed. No hostile signals. Yellow: owner names confirmed via local press, not on official website.

Who owns / operates it
