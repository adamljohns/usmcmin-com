Stockyards Restaurant & Bar

Bar & Restaurant · 407 William St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401

Verdict

Country-western bar and restaurant at 407 William St in downtown Fredericksburg, opened February 2026. A 10,000-square-foot entertainment venue developed by Darell Hertenstein (Hertenstein Investment Group). Features a 100-bottle bourbon vault, a mechanical bull named Buck Norris, line dancing on weeknights, and live country and rock music on weekends. Food menu includes nachos, wings, and smoked brisket sandwiches. Alcohol is the primary draw; not a family-first venue. No faith-hostile brand signals detected, but the bourbon and nightlife-centered brand is secular entertainment. Owner is an investment developer rather than a personal hands-on operator.

Who owns / operates it

Darell Hertenstein and Hertenstein Investment Group developed and own the space at 407-409 William St. Hertenstein has done development projects in King George and Westmoreland counties. The official site is vibespot.bar. No individual hands-on day-to-day operator has been publicly named beyond the Hertenstein investment group connection, making this investor-owned rather than personal owner-operated.
