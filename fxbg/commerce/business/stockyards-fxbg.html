Stockyards Restaurant & Bar

restaurant-bar · 407 William St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 670-2884
2 Green · 5 Yellow · 3 Gray

Verdict

Country-western bar and restaurant at 407 William St in downtown Fredericksburg, opened February 2026. 10,000-square-foot entertainment venue developed by Darell Hertenstein (Hertenstein Investment Group). Features a 100-bottle bourbon vault, a mechanical bull (Buck Norris), line dancing on weeknights, and live country and rock music on weekends. Food menu includes nachos, wings, and smoked brisket sandwiches. Alcohol-primary adult venue — not family-first. No faith-hostile signals detected. Owner is an investment developer rather than a personal hands-on operator. New business with a limited track record as of mid-2026.

Who owns / operates it

Darell Hertenstein (Hertenstein Investment Group, developer-owner)
