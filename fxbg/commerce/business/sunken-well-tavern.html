Sunken Well Tavern

restaurant · 720 Littlepage St · Fredericksburg · VA · 22401 · (540) 370-0911
5 Green · 2 Yellow · 3 Gray

Verdict

Neighborhood restaurant and tavern at 720 Littlepage St, operating since 2006 under co-owners Steve Cameli and Paul Stoddard. Located in a historic building (formerly Geo. Freeman & Sons grocery) steps from the Sunken Road battlefield and National Cemetery. Kitchen sources fresh produce from Snead’s Asparagus Farm, Glenburnie Farm, Miller Farm, and Moss Neck Farm. New American menu; hours 11am–9pm weekdays, 9am–9pm weekends. 399 Yelp reviews (updated June 2026). Farm partnerships and 20-year local presence are strong community fruit signals. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals. Tavern/bar component makes Family Safety Yellow.

Who owns / operates it

Steve Cameli and Paul Stoddard (co-owners since 2006)

Network path

Steve Cameli and Paul Stoddard are confirmed co-owners with 20 years of local presence and active farm-sourcing relationships. Farm-sourced New American menu in a relaxed neighborhood setting — solid lunch or dinner recommendation for brothers seeking quality local food. Tavern component means alcohol is present; not the default table for men watching alcohol. Faith unconfirmed — confirm before C5iSR intro.
