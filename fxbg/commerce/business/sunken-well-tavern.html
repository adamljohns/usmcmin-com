Sunken Well Tavern

Neighborhood Restaurant · 720 Littlepage St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 370-0911

Verdict

Neighborhood restaurant and tavern at 720 Littlepage St, operating since 2006 under co-owners Steve Cameli and Paul Stoddard. Located in a historic building that once housed Geo. Freeman & Sons grocery, steps from the Sunken Road battlefield and National Cemetery. The kitchen sources fresh produce from Snead's Asparagus Farm, Glenburnie Farm, Miller Farm, and Moss Neck Farm. New American menu with hearty portions; hours 11am–9pm weekdays, 9am–9pm weekends. 399 Yelp reviews (updated June 2026). Farm partnerships and 20-year local presence are strong community fruit signals. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals. Tavern/bar component makes Family Safety Yellow.

Who owns / operates it

Steve Cameli and Paul Stoddard are the confirmed co-owners of Sunken Well Tavern, operating together since the restaurant opened in 2006. The building at 720 Littlepage Street carries its own history — originally home to Geo. Freeman & Sons, a family-run provisions business, the structure itself has long served the neighborhood. Cameli and Stoddard have sustained the restaurant's local-sourcing model over two decades, maintaining farm relationships with multiple Fredericksburg-area producers.
