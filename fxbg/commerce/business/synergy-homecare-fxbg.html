SYNERGY HomeCare of Fredericksburg

In-Home Senior & Family Care · 500 Westwood Office Park, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (571) 752-2991

Verdict

Locally owned franchise location of SYNERGY HomeCare serving Fredericksburg and surrounding counties. Owner Beata Alghabra opened the franchise after a personal experience during COVID: when a hospital could not discharge her father without in-home care in place, she witnessed first-hand the gap in available services. She and her husband Jalal Alghabra (PA-C, 20+ years as a healthcare provider) operate the business together. Services include in-home senior care, post-surgery assistance, child care, and backup care. Chamber of Commerce member; seniorcarefinder.com listing updated January 2026; Birdeye profile active with 17 reviews. Franchise: SYNERGY national brand + Alghabra local operation; local operator judgment applies. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding; franchise wrapper introduces national brand variability, though local operator is mission-focused.

Who owns / operates it

Beata Alghabra owns and operates SYNERGY HomeCare of Fredericksburg as a local franchise. She comes from a background in healthcare management and finance. Her husband, Jalal Alghabra, is a PA-C with more than 20 years of clinical experience and works alongside her in the business. Beata's motivation for starting the business is documented publicly: during COVID, her father was kept in the hospital longer than necessary because no in-home care could be arranged, and watching that avoidable decline shaped her mission. Ownership and local operator confirmed via the Fredericksburg Chamber directory, About Us page, FXBG Neighbors feature, and SeniorCareFinder.
