Verdict

Locally owned franchise location of SYNERGY HomeCare serving Fredericksburg and surrounding counties. Owner Beata Alghabra opened the franchise after a personal COVID-era experience: her father was kept hospitalized longer than necessary because no in-home care could be arranged, motivating her to fill that gap. She and her husband Jalal Alghabra (PA-C, 20+ years clinical experience) operate the business together. Services include in-home senior care, post-surgery assistance, child care, and backup care. Chamber of Commerce member; SeniorCareFinder listing updated January 2026; Birdeye profile active. Franchise: SYNERGY national brand with Alghabra local operation; local operator judgment applies. No hostile signals. Yellow: no explicit faith branding; franchise wrapper introduces national brand variability.
