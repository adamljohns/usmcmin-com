In their own words

“We are Tales and Tails an independent bookstore cafe located at 1010 Charles Street, in Historic Downtown Fredericksburg. We have combined our love for books, dogs, and coffee into a space where all are welcome.”

Quoted from the business's website, checked 2026-10-06. Their description, not our review.

Verdict

Independent bookstore and coffee cafe in Historic Downtown Fredericksburg, opened May 2025 by Christine Bowman (ER physician) and Hunter Bufkin (former North Stafford HS teacher). All-ages general-interest inventory with dog-themed books and merchandise, locally roasted coffee, pastries, rentable study and meeting rooms, and a dog-friendly patio with a stick library and tennis balls. Active community partner: hosts book clubs, author events, and animal adoption events with the Fredericksburg SPCA and Old Dominion Humane Society. Yelp updated July 2026 (33 photos); Fredericksburg Regional Chamber member. No hostile signals. Yellow: excellent local ownership and community roots, no explicit faith branding.

Who owns / operates it

Christine Bowman (ER physician at a Fredericksburg-area hospital) and Hunter Bufkin (former schoolteacher; taught eight years at North Stafford High School). Husband-and-wife team.
