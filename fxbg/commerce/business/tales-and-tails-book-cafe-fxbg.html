Tales and Tails Book Cafe

Independent Bookstore & Cafe · 1010 Charles St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 870-7605

Verdict

Independent bookstore and coffee cafe in Historic Downtown Fredericksburg, opened May 2025 by Christine Bowman (ER physician) and Hunter Bufkin (former North Stafford HS teacher). All-ages general-interest inventory with dog-themed books and merchandise, locally roasted coffee, pastries, rentable study and meeting rooms, and a dog-friendly patio with a stick library and tennis balls. Active community partner: hosts book clubs, author events, and animal adoption events with the Fredericksburg SPCA and Old Dominion Humane Society. Yelp updated July 2026; Fredericksburg Regional Chamber member. No hostile signals. Yellow: excellent local ownership and community roots, no explicit faith branding.

Who owns / operates it

Christine Bowman and Hunter Bufkin — a husband-and-wife team — opened Tales and Tails on May 24, 2025. Christine is an emergency room physician working at a Fredericksburg-area hospital; Hunter taught school for eight years at North Stafford High School before joining Christine to build this venture. Their vision: a place that combines Christine's love of reading as escape with Hunter's classroom-honed sense of community, unified around books, coffee, and dogs. They partner with the Fredericksburg SPCA and Old Dominion Humane Society to host rescue animal adoption events on the dog-friendly patio.
