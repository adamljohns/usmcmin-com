Tapa Rio

Verdict

Family-owned riverside Latin and Italian tapas restaurant in downtown Fredericksburg. Owned and operated by the Acosta family — Erika Acosta serves as GM/proprietor; her parents Jose and Tania Acosta worked in chain restaurant kitchens for years before founding Tapa Rio in 2019, bringing their Salvadoran culinary heritage to the Rappahannock waterfront. Rated 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor with 172+ reviews; active May 2026. No hostile signals. Yellow: family ownership confirmed, strong community dining presence, no faith branding — verify before network introduction.
