Tapa Rio

Restaurant · Fredericksburg, VA · (540) 373-0870

Verdict

Family-owned riverside Latin and Italian tapas restaurant in downtown Fredericksburg. Owned and operated by the Acosta family — Erika Acosta serves as GM/proprietor; her parents Jose and Tania Acosta worked in chain restaurant kitchens for years before founding Tapa Rio in 2019, bringing their Salvadoran culinary heritage to the Rappahannock waterfront. The restaurant features tapas-style shared plates, housemade pizza, paella, and an emphasis on fresh ingredients. Rated 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor with 172+ reviews; confirmed active May 2026. No hostile signals. Yellow: family ownership confirmed, strong community dining presence, no faith branding in either direction — verify before network introduction.

Who owns / operates it

Acosta family — Erika Acosta is GM/proprietor and grew up cooking alongside her grandmother, then entered the restaurant world through her parents. Jose Acosta (father) worked for years as a kitchen manager in chain restaurants; Tania Acosta (mother) worked as a prep cook. The family is originally from El Salvador. They founded Tapa Rio in 2019 at 1101 Sophia Street, overlooking the Rappahannock River. Erika oversees day-to-day operations and carries the family's commitment to hospitality.
