Verdict

Locally owned title insurance agency founded in 2001 by Mark Steele, a Virginia Certified Title Examiner (VCTE) with nearly 40 years of industry experience. 1015 Prince Edward St, Fredericksburg VA 22401. Two-office operation serving Fredericksburg, Stafford, Spotsylvania, Prince William, King George, Caroline, Orange, Westmoreland, and Fauquier Counties. Member of Fredericksburg Regional Chamber of Commerce and Fredericksburg Area Builders Association (FABA). Old Republic Title Shining Star Award for Operational Excellence five consecutive years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. No hostile signals.
