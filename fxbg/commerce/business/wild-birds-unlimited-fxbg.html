Wild Birds Unlimited

Verdict

Locally franchised backyard birding and nature specialty store at 1937 Carl D Silver Pkwy in the Central Park shopping district. Owned by Tabatha Hummel, Caitlin Foschini, and Daniel Bender. Wild Birds Unlimited is a national franchise (300+ locations). Fredericksburg franchise is active and listed on the City of Fredericksburg Economic Development directory and Central Park retail directory. Yelp updated February 2026. Family-friendly specialty retail. Rated yellow: franchise model limits community-fruit and network-worthiness scores; local ownership confirmed via aggregators.

Who owns / operates it

Tabatha Hummel, Caitlin Foschini, and Daniel Bender, franchise owners. Tabatha Hummel formerly owned a pet store in Staunton, VA; relocated to Fredericksburg during the Covid-19 pandemic.

Network path

Verify-first for bird feeders, seed, and nature gifts. Good specialty niche — nothing else like it in FXBG. Locally operated by Tabatha Hummel and family. Check with them directly to confirm local culture before an intro. 1937 Carl D Silver Pkwy (Central Park). (540) 548-9393 · fredericksburg.wbu.com
