Wine & Design Fredericksburg

Arts & Entertainment · 502 Sophia St, Fredericksburg, VA 22401 · (540) 809-0899

Verdict

Locally operated paint-and-sip studio at 502 Sophia St in downtown Fredericksburg. Michelle Flynn opened this Wine & Design franchise in 2014, bringing 15 years of restaurant and construction industry experience. Classes run seven days a week guided by local artists, with canvases and paints provided. Private parties and corporate team-building events are available. The studio has operated continuously for over 10 years. Alcohol is served; classes are adult-oriented. Faith unconfirmed; no hostile brand signals.

Who owns / operates it

Michelle Flynn is the publicly named franchise owner of Wine & Design Fredericksburg. She opened the downtown Sophia Street location in 2014 after seeing the format succeed with friends who owned studios in Charlottesville and Richmond. Flynn's background spans 15 years in restaurant and construction industries before she transitioned to the creative arts and events space. The location is run independently under the national Wine & Design franchise system.
