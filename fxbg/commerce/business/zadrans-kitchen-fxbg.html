Who owns / operates it

Chef Humayun Zadran is the sole owner and executive chef. An Afghan refugee, he spent much of his early life in the Peshawar region of Pakistan before coming to the United States. He has more than 20 years of culinary experience, including earlier restaurant work in Kabul. He built Zadran's Kitchen through catering and local farmers market appearances before opening the Wolfe Street storefront in downtown Fredericksburg. The Fredericksburg Free Press described his philosophy as hospitality measured "by the length of a grain of rice."
