Methodology

10-Point Scorecard

Gather public evidence → score ten dimensions → apply hard gates → set overall band → publish sources → accept feedback.

Hard gates

  • Any Black-class finding → overall Black
  • Strong fail on Christ-centered public brand with clear activist/hostile identity → no higher than Red unless publicly reversed with current evidence
  • Insufficient evidence on ownership + brand + reliability → Gray, not guessed Green
  • Adam connection / visited flags never auto-Green a Red brand

Evidence rules

  1. Red/Black claims need URLs or dated public documents.
  2. No private accusations as score input.
  3. Score business public brand and offer, not employees’ private lives.
  4. Franchise: local operator + national brand both described; local page wins when they diverge.
  5. Corrections via feedback form; target review SLA 10 business days when evidence is provided.
