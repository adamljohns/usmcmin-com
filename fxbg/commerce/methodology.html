Evidence rules

  1. Red/Black require URLs or dated public documents.
  2. No private accusations as score input.
  3. Score public brand and offer, not employees' private lives.
  4. Franchise: local operator + national brand both described.
  5. Each factor shows source count or “insufficient evidence.”
  6. Appeals via feedback form; 10 business-day SLA when evidence provided.
