How to testify / write

Hearings, not hallway

Use the Hearing Management System. Open the hearing, then Register to Testify. Committees take live in-person or live virtual testimony. Written-only testimony uses Submit Testimony on the same calendar entry. The hearing notice sets the record deadline.

What to bring
