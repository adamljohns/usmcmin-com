How to speak / write

In the chamber

Formal, informal, standing-committee, budget, and special meetings are open in person. Public comment is usually the last part of the public-recognition block. Clerk rules: 30 minutes total, three minutes a speaker, on topics that are not already on that night’s agenda. Public hearings are required for ordinances and resolutions. An ordinance hearing must wait at least seven days after introduction.

Remote / watch

Microsoft Teams appears on the meeting agenda when offered. Formal meetings also air live on VPM/WCVW. Use the current agenda for the link — agendas get revised. Clerk informational line cited on the city site: 804-646-7955.

Council-Mayor. Council makes law and budget. The Mayor oversees the Chief Administrative Officer. Nine four-year district seats. President and Vice President are chosen by Council for two-year terms.
