How to speak / write

In the chamber

Public comment: sign up with the Clerk of Council before the meeting starts. Five minutes. Name, address, ZIP. City business only. Public hearing: five minutes at the podium when the floor opens.

Remote / written

Mail or email the Clerk of Council. Must arrive by 1:30 p.m. on the meeting date. Hearing letters must be clearly identified. Letters read aloud get three minutes.
