How to speak / write

In the chamber

Public comment: sign up with the Clerk before the meeting starts. Five minutes. Name, address, ZIP. City business only. Public hearing: no pre-signup; five minutes at the podium when the floor opens.

Remote / written

Mail or email the Clerk. Must arrive by 1:30 p.m. on the meeting date — the Aug 25 packet restates that clock. Hearing letters must be identified. Read-aloud = three minutes; 40-minute cap on read comment letters.
