Prayer Lists

A man will pray for “my family” for twenty years and never once pray for his wife by name about the thing she is actually carrying. These lists exist to stop that. Put your names in, and the prayers become yours — on the screen and in the printed copy.

How the personalised copy works

Open a list, type your names, and tap Make my PDF. Your browser’s print dialog opens on a clean booklet with a cover page — choose Save as PDF (on iPhone: Share, then Print, then Save to Files).

Your names never leave your phone. There is no account, no server, and nothing uploaded — they are stored on your own device and we never see them.
