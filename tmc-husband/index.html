How it works

  • 1 Read the module

    Short opening, Scripture anchor, and 3–5 numbered tasks. About 45–60 minutes.

  • 2 Ship the field action

    One required exercise with a visible finish line. Husband-owned — her participation is invited, not demanded.

  • 3 Optional depth

    Study aids from Notebook by Gemini, a knowledge check, and conversation prompts if she is willing.

Before you begin

This course offers education and practice for a basically safe marriage. It is not counseling or crisis care. If there is fear, coercion, violence, stalking, sexual pressure, active addiction, or immediate danger, prioritize confidential individual support rather than a joint exercise.
