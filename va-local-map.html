How this map is computed

  • Each person's number is the same 0–100 headline score as on their profile page.
  • Only evidence-scored, currently sitting local officials count. A name with no researched record does not pull a county down.
  • A county or independent city shades only when at least three officials are scored. Most counties currently have a sheriff record only, so they stay grey. That is missing research, not a grade.
  • Combined jurisdictions (a county paired with a city on one record) are left off until they can be split cleanly.
