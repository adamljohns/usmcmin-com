Heritage says Canada’s assisted-suicide system has claimed more than 100,000 lives

Tuesday’s commentary says that since 2016, assisted suicide has claimed more than 100,000 Canadian lives. It cites a 26-year-old with depression who shopped for a doctor to sign off on his death, and a disabled man who applied for medical assistance in dying because he feared going homeless. France fires an upgraded nuclear-capable missile from a submarine

Defense News reported Tuesday that the nuclear-powered ballistic-missile submarine Le Vigilant fired an M51.3 in an acceptance test after the boat’s overhaul. The missile carried no nuclear warhead. It launched off Finistère in western France and splashed down in the North Atlantic. President Macron called the shot a test of France’s strategic credibility and watched from a Navy vessel.

The question it poses is what moral framework could make that celebration look like justice. It is an argument about American politics, not a new police finding.

Tech / AI

Pentagon names four directed-energy weapons for a base counter-drone pilot

Defense News reported Tuesday that the Pentagon on Monday named four systems for a homeland counter-drone pilot Congress ordered: AeroVironment’s Palletized High-Energy Laser, Epirus’ Leonidas high-powered microwave, Kord’s Firefly high-energy laser, and Boeing’s Compact Laser Weapon System. Joint Interagency Task Force 401 will field them at U.S. bases. Army Brig. Gen. Matt Ross said the year-long pilot is meant to show how to integrate and sustain the weapons, not to declare them ready.

Defense News

53rd Wing testers ran Black Flag with the Navy at Point Mugu

DVIDS reported Tuesday that the 85th and 422nd Test and Evaluation Squadrons, both in the 53rd Wing, tested emerging capabilities with joint and allied partners at Naval Base Ventura County from Sept. 10 to 25. The event ran with the Navy’s Gray Flag exercise so units could share range time. Capt. Kaleb Nypaver, 85th TES director of operations, said the point was a test no one squadron could run at home station.
