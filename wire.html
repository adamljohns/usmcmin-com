Destroyer USS Spruance passes 342 days at sea, the longest Navy destroyer deployment since World War II

Defense News reported Oct. 29 that the Arleigh Burke-class destroyer had reached 342 days deployed after leaving San Diego with the Abraham Lincoln carrier strike group on Nov. 21, 2025. Navy leaders say extended deployments will remain common while ships returning from operations enter a maintenance system already carrying years of accumulated delays.

Pentagon officials ask Baltic allies to temper public rhetoric about Russia

Defense News and Reuters report that U.S. officials privately urged Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia to use more measured language as Washington renews its push for negotiations over Ukraine. Hanwha's Philadelphia yard wins the first Landing Ship Medium construction award

USNI News reports that TOTE Services awarded Hanwha Philly Shipyard a $269 million contract to build the Marine Corps' Landing Ship Medium. TOTE holds the Navy's $2.2 billion vessel-construction-manager contract for the program.

France turns tank gunners into FPV pilots and plans 15 drone-combat units by summer 2027

France's 1st Marine Infantry Regiment converted a light-cavalry squadron into a dedicated drone unit after exercises showed small FPV teams could disrupt armored formations and command posts. Army leaders are scaling the model while warning that weather, jamming, and a lack of riflemen still limit drone-only formations.

Ukraine finishes its share of Freyja missile defense as European components become the bottleneck

Ukraine says its FP-7.X interceptor and launcher work is complete, while partner companies continue integrating radars, seekers, fire control, and a command center. The coalition is aiming for initial tests by year's end, but the seeker remains the hardest piece and battlefield use is targeted for next July.

The essay argues that inexpensive, attritable drones can spare troops while lowering the public and political cost of continuing a conflict.
