A retired Marine drill instructor went back to college so his daughter could finish Vanderbilt

Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division said Thursday that Dwayne Fochler, a retired Marine, enrolled at the University of La Verne in 2018 after he learned he could not transfer GI Bill benefits to his daughter as a retiree. The housing allowance and book stipend went to her. He finished a bachelor's degree in 2020. She earned back what Vanderbilt cost within five years. Fochler now heads the Targets and Mission Planning Support Division at Point Mugu and received a 40-year Length-of-Service Award on Sept. 30. The official still, taken Oct. 21 and posted today, shows him with a BQM-177A aerial target in a hangar.

Theodore Roosevelt and allied ships are in the Philippine Sea for Keen Sword

U.S. Forces Japan said Keen Sword 27 opened Oct. 19. About 45,000 Japan Self-Defense Force personnel and 11,000 U.S. troops are in the exercise, with 260 aircraft and 40 ships, including the Theodore Roosevelt Carrier Strike Group. Australia, Canada, France, New Zealand, and the Philippines are in the drills. Korea and the United Kingdom sent observers. USNI News reported Thursday that the strike group is sailing with Japanese, Australian, Canadian, and French warships in the Philippine Sea. The exercise runs through Oct. 29.

The Pentagon and Lockheed agreed to a 10-year frame for at least 850 F-35s

Defense News reported Thursday that the Pentagon and Lockheed Martin agreed to a decade-long framework covering at least 850 F-35 Lightning II fighters for the U.S. military. The buy still needs congressional approval and funding. The story does not reprint the contract.

Defense News

Army maintenance software can now keep working offline for seven days

The Army said Thursday that the Global Combat Support System-Army's disconnected maintenance tool, DISCOPS, lets units keep essential work going for up to seven days when the network is down. Records sync when the link returns. The release calls it an operational milestone for units that cannot stop maintenance because the connection dropped.
