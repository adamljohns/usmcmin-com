The Army stood up the 4th Multi-Domain Task Force at Fort Carson

The 4th Multi-Domain Task Force was activated Oct. 8 at Founder's Field on Fort Carson, Colorado. Col. Hugh W. A. Jones took command of a formation the Army says will include nearly 2,100 soldiers and report to the 7th Infantry Division at Joint Base Lewis-McChord. The unit is built to synchronize long-range precision fires against anti-access networks, with an operational focus on the Indo-Pacific. It follows the 1st task force in Washington state, the 2nd in Germany, and the 3rd in Hawaii. A fifth is planned for Fort Bragg. Jones told the formation that if war comes, the unit must be ready to fight and win.

American Reformer says politics is a place, not a purity test

Law & Liberty says a forgotten Washington statue in Budapest was paid for by immigrants

Friday's essay says the George Washington statue beside a lake in Budapest's City Park was commissioned and financed by Hungarian immigrants in the United States, not by a treaty between governments. The Paris and London statues were diplomatic gifts. This one was raised by people who had left Hungary. The writer puts the story against the current argument over immigration and assimilation, on the 250th anniversary of the country's birth. It is an essay, not a bill.

Tech / AI

Estonia's KrattWorks is building a Shahed-like dummy drone for target practice

Defense News reported Friday from Warsaw that KrattWorks is developing an aerial target meant to mimic jet-powered Shahed-type drones so crews can practice shooting them down. Business development manager Ranno Pajuri said a working prototype is expected by the end of 2027, and that the software can move to different hardware more easily than the airframe. The company already sells one-way attack drones and the Ghost Dragon ISR drone, and has supplied Ukraine. It is promoting the line at AUSA in Washington. A demonstration date is not posted.

Defense News

I MEF planners spent the week with Philippine counterparts on crisis response

A DVIDS release says Marines with I Marine Expeditionary Force and Marine Rotational Force–Southeast Asia joined Philippine Joint Task Force personnel for a planning exercise from Oct. 5 to Oct. 9. Col. Robert S. Bunn, commanding officer of the rotational force, said the week built on months of work across the archipelago. Planners worked a scenario on authorities, command relationships, and how the two forces would coordinate in a crisis. The Philippine side led. No new treaty or deployment order is in the release.
