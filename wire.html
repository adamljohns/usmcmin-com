Iran strike in Jordan leaves one A-10 without a wing; eight F-15s back in service after light damage

Defense News, Thursday: a U.S. official speaking on condition of anonymity to Reuters said Iranian strikes on Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan left one A-10 Thunderbolt II with a missing wing, while roughly eight F-15s took light damage and were returned to service. The A-10 is the hard loss; the F-15s were not written off. The same report cites a May Congressional Research Service count of 42 U.S. aircraft lost or damaged in the conflict, and 18 U.S. service members killed with more than 800 injured since February. No DoD on-record statement.
