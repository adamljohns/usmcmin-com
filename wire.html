USNI: Hormuz tanker transits rose more than 30 percent last week

USNI News, Friday evening: transits through the Strait of Hormuz rose more than 30 percent last week, led mostly by tankers and gas carriers. Lloyd’s List Intelligence counted 114 transits between Aug. 17 and Sunday, compared with the Aug. 10–16 week. The count is still below pre-war levels and is likely to move as more dark tankers are identified. The item is a weekly traffic tally, not a Navy declaration that the strait is open.

Navy accepts the last Expeditionary Sea Base, named for a Marine

Navy press release, Wednesday: the service accepted delivery of USNS Hector A. Cafferata Jr. (ESB 8) from General Dynamics NASSCO in San Diego on Aug. 25, after integrated sea trials this summer. The ship honors Marine Pfc. Hector A. Cafferata Jr., Medal of Honor at Chosin Reservoir. It is the sixth and final ESB. The Navy has not named an operating area.

OpenAI letter: a limited window to strengthen cyber defense

OpenAI published an open letter Thursday calling for collective action on cyber defense as AI-enabled attacks get cheaper. CNBC counted 116 companies and entities on the roster, including Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and AMD. The letter asks governments to fund defense for hospitals and water plants. It is a joint appeal, not a statute.

CISA’s NetScaler patch deadline for federal agencies is today

CISA on Wednesday added six flaws to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including CVE-2026-8452 in Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway. Binding Operational Directive 26-04 tells federal civilian agencies to remediate listed high-risk holes on publicly exposed assets. SecurityWeek reported the federal due date as Aug. 29. CISA encourages other operators to treat the catalog the same way; the directive itself binds federal agencies.
