SOUTHCOM: four killed in a Caribbean go-fast strike

Defense News: Italy asks Brussels for €8 billion in defense loans

Defense News, Wednesday from Rome: Italy asked the EU’s SAFE fund for €8 billion in cheap defense loans, down from a planned €14.9 billion. A source familiar with the talks confirmed the request. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni’s coalition had delayed for months. The report does not say when Brussels will decide.

Defense News: the Army wants 8,000 short-range missiles a year

Defense News, Wednesday: the Army posted a sources-sought notice for a Hellfire-class missile that can fire from an M299 or equivalent launcher. Industry replies are due Sept. 15. After award, the first batch of up to 8,000 rounds is due in two years, then up to 8,000 a year. The notice wants an active production line.

UK: MBDA may share SCALP parts data so Ukraine can assemble

The British government said Monday that MBDA may release classified data on U.K. parts of the SCALP cruise missile so Ukraine and France can assemble the weapon in Ukraine. Defense News, Wednesday: Kyiv now has the licenses, but wartime factories are unlikely to turn out large numbers soon. No first-delivery date was published.
