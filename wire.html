The Marine Corps has rolled out new tiered water survival qualification levels fleet-wide, replacing the old standard with a minimum requirement that every Marine complete a 50-meter self-rescue and tread water for five minutes before using gear as flotation

USNI News reports the Marine Corps set new minimum fleet water survival requirements under a policy released this month. Marine Corps Times, citing the Nov. 16 Marine Administrative Message implementing the change (effective Oct. 1), details five new qualification levels: Water Survival Basic remains the one-year recruit-graduation minimum; Water Survival Novice is the new fleet-wide minimum, requiring a 50-meter self-rescue, at least one swimming stroke, a five-minute 'stay at the surface' exercise, shedding gear in shallow water and a 25-meter swim using flotation gear, valid two years; Water Survival Competent is a new total-force qualification requiring a 150-meter self-rescue, two strokes, a seven-minute surface exercise, shedding gear in deep water and the same 25-meter flotation swim, valid three years; and Water Survival Proficient replaces the old Intermediate level at a 250-meter standard. The service said existing testing uniforms, equipment and procedures are unchanged, including the long-standing 'stay at the surface' portion using a blouse or trousers as improvised flotation. news.usni.org 403'd (Cloudflare 'Just a moment' challenge) on same-turn fetch, so qualification-level detail comes from a same-turn clean (200) fetch of Marine Corps Times quoting the Nov. 16 MARADMIN directly, not invented.

America's political obsession with "authenticity" has curdled into something worse than ordinary rule-breaking, a new essay argues: a shamelessness in which public figures refuse to even admit a moral line exists

The essay contrasts the measured politician dismissed as fake with the one who flouts public conventions and is celebrated as real, naming examples: commentator Nick Fuentes' remark that Hitler was "really... cool," streamer Hasan Piker's comment that America deserved the Sept. 11 attacks, and Rep. Jasmine Crockett's mockery of Texas Gov. Greg Abbott's wheelchair use as "Governor Hot Wheels," after which she refused to apologize, asking a reporter, "Why would I apologize when I put out a statement?" The author argues a person can act shamefully while still recognizing standards matter, but the modern chase for authenticity has turned ordinary rule-breaking into something worse: an unwillingness to admit a line was ever there to cross.

Tech / AI

France launched a new defense-industry pact this week to build up counter-drone capability, after repeated drone incursions over a nuclear submarine base and a naval base exposed gaps the country's junior armed forces minister called a "quite brutal reality check"

Defense News reports the pact, unveiled by defense-industry association Gicat chairman Emmanuel Levacher at a presentation outside Paris, adds a counter-drone dimension to a drone-industry pact France launched in 2024 and will run through summer 2027, pairing defense firms directly with military end users to cut procurement red tape. Junior armed forces minister Alice Rufo said France has already faced problems from insufficient counter-drone capability, without detailing them, citing drone overflights of the Île Longue nuclear submarine base in December 2025 and the Toulon naval base this past October, plus French media reports of drones over a Bergerac explosives plant and a freight train carrying Leclerc tanks near Mulhouse in November 2025. The new pact covers systems to counter aerial, maritime and ground drones alike.

Defense News

Leonardo has rolled out the first of 15 new M-346 jets built to take over stunt-flying duty for the Italian Air Force's Frecce Tricolori aerobatic team, replacing aircraft that have flown the role for more than 40 years

Defense News reports the display-configured M-346s will be delivered to the Frecce Tricolori between 2027 and 2028, replacing the MB-339 PAN that has flown the aerobatic role for four decades; Leonardo says the newer jet's greater maneuverability will allow for better displays. The aircraft will be upgraded with a smoke-generation system, video-streaming capability, avionics modifications and auxiliary fuel tanks. Leonardo's aircraft-division managing director Stefano Bortoli said the program positions the M-346 for further commercial and industrial opportunities worldwide as both a trainer and an aerobatic-team platform; the company has now sold more than 170 M-346s to 10 countries, including trainer-version customers Italy, Singapore, Israel, Poland, Qatar and Greece, and light-fighter customers Turkmenistan, Nigeria, Austria and, most recently, Indonesia.

Three 19th-century British conservatives — poet Robert Southey, writer Thomas Carlyle and statesman Benjamin Disraeli — get a fresh look in a new essay testing whether their warnings about the Industrial Revolution's machines can sharpen today's debate over artificial intelligence

The essay recounts how Southey, England's poet laureate from 1813 to 1843, feared factories would destroy workers' independence and moral autonomy and breed urban poverty and political radicalism; how Carlyle worried society was being remade in the machine's image, organized around calculation rather than virtue; and how Disraeli, more measured, sought not to halt industrialization but to bind social classes together through reciprocal obligation rather than bare contract, becoming a forerunner of "one-nation conservatism." The essay asks whether today's AI skeptics are drawing on the same real dangers these Victorian conservatives identified, or whether today's technology enthusiasts are right to dismiss the comparison.

The Apple TV+ film "Way of the Warrior Kid," based on former Navy SEAL Jocko Willink's bestselling novel, aims at a classic boy's coming-of-age story starring Chris Pratt but settles for the bare minimum, a review finds

The review follows Marc (Jude Hill), an 11-year-old bullied by classmates and behind in academics, athletics and friendships, whose injured Uncle Jake (Chris Pratt), a decorated Navy SEAL recuperating at Marc's home, devises "Operation Warrior Kid," a program built on SEAL training that teaches real courage rather than fighting technique while Jake confronts his own demons. A line of "scandalous women" runs through the Bible from Tamar and Rahab to Ruth and Bathsheba, and the Gospel's opening scandal — Joseph's discovery that his betrothed Mary is pregnant — belongs to that same venerable, unlikely company, a new essay argues

The essay recalls that when Joseph learned Mary was pregnant he planned to quietly set her aside rather than publicly humiliate her, until an angel assured him the child was the Spirit's gift and the fulfillment of prophecy — yet the scandal lingered, with Jesus's own accusers later throwing his parentage back in his face. Matthew's genealogy, the essay notes, seasons Jesus's lineage with disreputable women: Tamar, who posed as a prostitute to secure sons in Abraham's line; Rahab, a Jericho prostitute who turned traitor to protect Israel's spies; Ruth the Moabite, kin to the women who seduced Israel into idolatry; and Bathsheba, David's adulterous partner. The essay observes the Bible nonetheless commends each woman — Judah calls Tamar "more righteous than I," and Boaz blesses Ruth — arguing Mary's scandal fulfills rather than breaks that pattern.
