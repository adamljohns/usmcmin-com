U.S. and Indian armies closed three weeks of training in India

DVIDS, posted Sunday from Auli: U.S. and Indian Army forces closed Exercise Yudh Abhyas 2026 in a ceremony Sept. 27, ending three weeks of bilateral training at Auli and Mahajan Field Firing Range. The 22nd iteration paired the 11th Airborne Division's 1st Mobile Brigade Combat Team with the Indian Army for jungle training, combined air assault, and live fire that included HIMARS. Col. Ben Jackman said the point was soldiers who know each other's equipment if they are ever called to fight together. Official still from the closing formation.

British police arrested five men near a U.S. bomber base in England

Defense News, Sunday afternoon, citing Reuters: British police arrested five men near RAF Fairford after a tip that three vans were heading toward the airfield, which hosts U.S. Air Force personnel and has been used to strike Iran. President Trump said the suspects had been looking to do big damage and had been under investigation by British and U.S. authorities. Counter Terrorism Policing said the five remain in custody on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act, and that police believe the incident is contained. A source familiar with the investigation told Reuters an Iranian-linked motive is considered the most likely. Arrest report, not a completed prosecution.
