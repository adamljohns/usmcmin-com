USNI: Navy finishes its first barracks overhaul under the housing push

USNI News, Thursday: the Navy finished the first of its modernization projects meant to move sailors into shore housing as part of the Pentagon’s Barracks Task Force. Installations command said the service renovated living quarters at Naval Base Kitsap-Bremerton and installed new furnishings. A Navy release on the same Bremerton unaccompanied-housing work said the 10-story, 152-room building now holds 304 beds and finished 30 days early on April 6, after fire and water damage in August 2025. One finished building starts the task force; the wider barracks problem is still open.

Navy accepts the last Expeditionary Sea Base, named for a Marine

Navy press release, Wednesday: the service accepted delivery of USNS Hector A. Cafferata Jr. (ESB 8) from General Dynamics NASSCO in San Diego on Aug. 25, after integrated sea trials this summer. The ship honors Marine Pfc. Hector A. Cafferata Jr., Medal of Honor at Chosin Reservoir. It is the sixth and final ESB. The Navy has not named an operating area.

USNI: Hormuz tanker transits rose more than 30 percent last week

USNI News, Friday evening: transits through the Strait of Hormuz rose more than 30 percent last week, led mostly by tankers and gas carriers. Lloyd’s List Intelligence counted 114 transits between Aug. 17 and Sunday, compared with the Aug. 10–16 week. The count is still below pre-war levels and is likely to move as more dark tankers are identified. The item is a weekly traffic tally, not a Navy declaration that the strait is open.

Google: AI Mode can track flight prices and book hotels

Google, Thursday: AI Mode in Search can now set flight-price alerts in the chat, show points or miles rates, and book some hotels through Google Pay. Flight tracking is listed in more than 180 countries. Hotel booking is rolling out in the U.S. in English with named partners including Booking.com, Expedia, Hilton, and Marriott. The hotel or booking platform remains the merchant of record. This is a company product post.

CISA: ownCloud and Linux kernel holes are due today for federal agencies

CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog lists CVE-2023-49105 in ownCloud and CVE-2026-53362 in the Linux kernel with a due date of Aug. 30. JFrog Artifactory CVE-2026-66384 is due Sept. 10. Binding Operational Directive 26-04 tells federal civilian agencies to prioritize listed high-risk holes on publicly exposed assets. The directive binds those agencies; CISA encourages other operators to treat the catalog the same way.
