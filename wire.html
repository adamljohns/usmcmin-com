Defense News: Ukraine asks the EU to fund its first PAC-3 Patriot buy

Defense News, Saturday: Kyiv asked to tap a €90 billion EU support loan for PAC-3 interceptors for Patriot batteries — the first formal request to use that loan for the U.S.-made missiles. Because PAC-3s are built in the United States, the purchase needs an exemption from the loan’s non-EU content cap. Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the review was moving and that EU representatives were due to decide Monday.

Defense News: Air Force speeds a cheaper Reaper successor after Iran losses

Defense News, Friday: Lt. Gen. Christopher Niemi said Sept. 3 the Air Force is accelerating a cheaper, more expendable follow-on to the MQ-9A Reaper after combat losses. Defense News: RAND says the U.S. lacks hands to rebuild wartime satellites

Defense News, Friday: RAND’s Aug. 31 report “Breaking Glass, Missing Hands” says the United States does not have enough engineers, technicians, and assemblers to rebuild military satellites in 12 to 24 months after a war with China or Russia. Gen. Michael Guetlein sponsored the study as vice chief of space operations.

AP: xAI sues Minnesota over the state’s AI nudification ban

Associated Press: xAI sued Monday over Minnesota’s first-in-the-nation ban on AI “nudification” tools, which takes effect Saturday.
