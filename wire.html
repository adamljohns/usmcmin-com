Theodore Roosevelt pulled into Yokosuka ahead of Keen Sword

The Navy said Friday that the aircraft carrier USS Theodore Roosevelt, flagship of Carrier Strike Group 9, arrived at Commander, Fleet Activities Yokosuka on Oct. 16 with the cruiser USS Chosin and the destroyer USS Decatur. The port call is for maintenance and resupply before Keen Sword, a routine biennial field exercise with Japan's Self-Defense Forces. Capt. Will Mathis, the carrier's commanding officer, said the visit lets the crew rest before operating alongside Japanese forces. Rear Adm. Marcos Jasso, the strike-group commander, called the U.S.-Japan alliance the cornerstone of peace and security in the region.

Heritage says a public school can be a fight for survival, not a place to learn

Friday's Heritage commentary quotes Evan Barker, writing in the London Times on Sept. 29, on a Kansas City, Missouri, school two decades ago: every day was a fight for survival, not a place to get an education. During passing periods, she wrote, she hurried to avoid being jumped. The piece asks what comes after a system that failed those kids.

Heritage says France's riots echo 1968, and Macron is no de Gaulle

Friday's Heritage commentary recalls March 29, 1968, when President Charles de Gaulle flew in secret to a French military base in Germany to learn whether the army would stay loyal as leftist protests crippled the country. The piece says today's French riots have strong similarities, and that Emmanuel Macron is not that leader.

Tech / AI

Hanwha says two lasers shot down three drones in a South Korean Army drill

Defense News reports that Hanwha Systems said its stationary Cheongwang laser and a 30-kilowatt vehicle-mounted laser shot down all three target drones at the Army's TIGER+ exercise on Oct. 14 in Yangpyeong. About 500 people attended, including Army Chief of Staff Gen. Kim Kyu-ha. Hanwha did not say which system hit which drone, what kinds of drones were used, or the range of either weapon. The company says each Cheongwang shot costs about $1.50.

Arizona will be the first Guard state to field the Gray Eagle 25M

Defense News reports that General Atomics said Monday the Arizona National Guard will get eight Gray Eagle 25M drones by September 2027 and four more by the end of 2029, the first Army National Guard company on the type. The company said Congress has funded three Guard units so far, and that the aircraft can support combatant commanders and defend against threats in the Western Hemisphere. The 25M first flew in December 2023. The photograph with the story is a company image, not an official still.

Law & Liberty reads Mangione through Crime and Punishment

Friday's Law & Liberty essay says Luigi Mangione, who recently admitted killing UnitedHealthcare chief Brian Thompson in 2024, reasoned the way Dostoevsky's Raskolnikov did: that one murder could be a social good. The piece quotes a diary line in which Mangione wrote he felt no doubt the killing was justified. It argues that no theory makes murder moral, and that Americans should not decide which lives are expendable for a world they want to build.
