An Aviano medic stopped at a wreck on the way to work and stayed until help arrived

The 31st Fighter Wing said Thursday that Tech. Sgt. Danielle Smith, a medical technician with the 31st Operational Medical Readiness Squadron, watched a car hit a utility pole and overturn on the morning of Aug. 26 near Aviano Air Base, Italy. She moved the driver clear of the wreck, got his permission, and checked him for major bleeding before local emergency crews arrived about ten minutes later. Authorities then requested aeromedical evacuation, and the driver was flown to a hospital with a possible chest injury and a latent head injury. He survived. Smith, who has been a medical technician for nearly 18 years, said being a medic is who she is when someone needs help. The official still, posted this morning, shows her outside the flight medicine building.

Law & Liberty counts the civic-center enrollments that legislatures built

Wednesday's Law & Liberty essay tracks public-university civic centers created by legislative or trustee action. Arizona State's School of Civic and Economic Thought and Leadership opened in 2017. Nineteen more have opened since 2021. It cites fall 2026 enrollments: Ohio State's Chase Center above 500, up from 28 last fall; Arizona State above 1,300; Tennessee's Institute of American Civics at 608; Texas's School of Civic Leadership at 1,683. The piece is argument about how those centers last. It does not reprint a bill.

Tech / AI

Air Force told Congress it needs 1,509 combat-coded fighters by 2035

Defense News reported Wednesday that a force-structure report sent to Congress on Oct. 16 sets the Air Force requirement at 1,509 combat-coded fighters by 2035. Lt. Gen. David Tabor, deputy chief of staff for plans and programs, described the mix at an Oct. 20 Pentagon roundtable: F-35s about 40 percent, up from about 30 percent now; F-22s and their replacement, the F-47, about 20 percent; F-15s and F-16s splitting the rest. A-10s are out of the 2035 plan. The 1,509 figure does not count Collaborative Combat Aircraft. Reporters were not given the report itself.

Defense News

The 101st Combat Aviation Brigade started a 32-week test of a VR cognitive trainer

The 101st Combat Aviation Brigade said Wednesday that it opened a 32-week pilot of the NeuroTrainer system on Oct. 20. The Department of War had named specialized units in July to test whether the virtual-reality cognitive trainer belongs in aviation training. The official account is the start of a test, not a finding that the system works.

NATO's procurement agency will let Ukraine buy through its catalog, the Financial Times reported

Defense News, citing Reuters and the Financial Times, reported Thursday that the NATO Support and Procurement Agency will let Ukraine buy weapons, ammunition, and equipment through its centralized catalog. Jacqueline Borsboom, the agency's director of support to operations, told the Financial Times the arrangement gives Kyiv a structured way into procurement expertise and the industrial base. The story is a report of an agency statement. It does not reprint a NATO decision text.
