Defense News: CBO puts Iran combat costs at $38 billion and interceptor stocks down

Defense News, Tuesday: the Congressional Budget Office estimates U.S. combat operations against Iran cost about $38 billion through Aug. 1 and consumed between one-half and two-thirds of the combined Patriot, THAAD, SM-3, and SM-6 interceptor inventory since June 2025.
