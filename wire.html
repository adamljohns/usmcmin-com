Defense News: F-16 crashes at Spangdahlem; pilot ejected and is in treatment

Defense News, Tuesday: an F-16 assigned to Spangdahlem Air Base in western Germany crashed Tuesday. The U.S. military said one pilot ejected safely and was receiving medical treatment, and that the cause is under investigation. German air traffic control said four F-16s were in a training exercise; one is believed to have crashed on approach, and the others landed at Ramstein.

Defense News: CENTCOM has become a battle lab for unmanned systems

Defense News, Tuesday: unmanned systems have moved from exercises into an active war. After a U.S. Army helicopter went down near the Strait of Hormuz in June, a Navy unmanned vessel helped recover two service members. A month later, CENTCOM said three Corsair sea drones struck a submarine and ship maintenance facility at Bandar Abbas — the first time American forces employed sea drones in combat. Field use, not a procurement decision.
