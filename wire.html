Navy closes fiscal 2026 with 45,112 enlisted recruits, past its 45,000 goal

The Navy brought in at least 45,112 active-duty enlisted sailors in fiscal 2026, according to final numbers the service released Thursday, USNI News reported. The Navy said in July it had already reached its goal of 45,000, three months ahead of schedule. Every one of those enlistees starts at Recruit Training Command in Great Lakes, Ill., the Navy’s only boot camp, where training runs about nine weeks. The Navy has not yet published how the new fiscal year’s goal compares.

Pacific allies weigh U.S. signals after Trump’s talks with Xi

Army leaders from 25 Indo-Pacific nations met in Auckland in late September to plan closer cooperation. In the same week, U.S. Ambassador David Perdue said President Trump had offered to sell weapons to China; Washington officials later said no such sale is planned. Larger arms packages for Taiwan remain on hold.

Taiwan takes delivery of its first two new F-16V Block 70 fighters

The jets landed Friday at Chihhang Air Base in Taitung after flying via Hawaii and Guam, the first of 66 ordered in 2019 under an $8 billion sale. Software problems delayed delivery. The Block 70 carries newer radar, avionics and weapons; Defense Minister Wellington Koo said more would arrive soon but gave no dates.
