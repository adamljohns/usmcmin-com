A French artillery regiment is splitting its guns into pairs to survive drones

Defense News, Tuesday, from Besançon: Col. Rémy Jaillet, who commands the 68th African Artillery Regiment, said the unit is reshuffling its 155 mm Caesar batteries from two four-gun sections into three two-gun sections. The fleet size stays the same for now. Jaillet said smaller, more dispersed units hold up better against counter-battery fire. He said the change is a trial at that regiment, not yet an Army-wide format.
