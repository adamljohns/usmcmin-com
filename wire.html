Misawa added an F-35 engine trainer so maintainers can drill without a live jet

DVIDS, posted overnight from Misawa Air Base: the 35th Fighter Wing added an F-35A Lightning II engine-run simulator so maintainers can train on aircraft systems without using a live jet. Instructors can run engine-fire and hydraulic-failure scenarios in a closed room. Staff Sgt. Joshua Owens said the trainer saves about $12,000 in fuel per class, or about $144,000 a year, and puts the aircraft back on the line. Official classes for maintainers begin in November. Official still from the trainer room, Sept. 24.

British police arrested five men near a U.S. bomber base in England

Defense News, Sunday, citing Reuters: British police arrested five men near RAF Fairford after a tip that three vans were heading toward the airfield, which hosts U.S. Air Force personnel and has been used to strike Iran. President Trump said the suspects had been looking to do big damage and had been under investigation by British and U.S. authorities. Counter Terrorism Policing said the five remain in custody on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act, and that police believe the incident is contained. A source familiar with the investigation told Reuters an Iranian-linked motive is considered the most likely. Arrest report, not a completed prosecution.
