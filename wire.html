USS John Basilone arrived in Rota, completing the shift from Mayport

A Navy photograph posted Wednesday shows a brow secured to the Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer USS John Basilone (DDG 122) after the ship arrived at Naval Station Rota, Spain. The caption says the arrival on Oct. 14 completed the homeport shift from Mayport, Florida. The photograph was taken Oct. 13 by Mass Communication Specialist 2nd Class Alyssa Sperle. Rota is the Navy's forward hub for destroyers covering Europe, Africa, and the Middle East.

Tech / AI

Kratos and Airbus flew the U740 Valkyrie combat drone over Europe

Defense News reports that Kratos and Airbus said the U740 Valkyrie completed a first European test flight this week, built for German and other European forces. The companies said the flight met its goals: a rocket-assisted takeoff, maneuvers at several altitudes, speeds up to Mach 0.8, and mission changes in flight. They described a 30-foot airframe with a 27-foot wingspan and an internal weapons bay.

Defense News

France will build its next fighter at home after the joint project with Germany collapsed

Defense News reports that Air and Space Force Chief of Staff Jérôme Bellanger told the National Assembly's defense committee on Wednesday that France now treats the future fighter, Avion de Combat du Futur, as a national program meant to fly conventional missions in 2040. France and Germany agreed in June to scrap the joint Future Combat Air System with Spain. Bellanger said Paris remains open to later cooperation that would not structure the aircraft's birth.

Leo called just war outdated because it needs rulers who will judge

Thursday's Providence essay says Pope Leo XIV's encyclical Magnifica Humanitas called just war theory outdated. The writer agrees, but not as a call to drop the tradition: just war is not self-executing. It assumes legitimate political authority, shared moral concepts, and people who will make the hard call.
