USS Howard drilled its small-caliber guns in the Northern Pacific

A Navy photograph posted Friday morning shows Chief Gunner's Mate Dalton Daniel explaining an M240 machine gun to the small-caliber action team on the starboard bridge wing of the Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer USS Howard (DDG 83). The caption says the ship was operating in the Northern Pacific on Oct. 14. Howard is forward-deployed with Destroyer Squadron 15, the Navy's largest destroyer squadron and 7th Fleet's principal surface force. The photograph was taken Oct. 13 by Mass Communication Specialist 1st Class Matt Riggs.

Tech / AI

Lockheed is putting $300 million into its Grand Prairie missile plant

Defense News reports that Lockheed Martin is investing $300 million to expand its Grand Prairie, Texas, plant, which supports every program in its Missiles and Fire Control business, including PAC-3 MSE, THAAD, the Precision Strike Missile, and the AIM-260. The company said Oct. 14 that the work will help the plant reach record production and feed final assembly in Camden, Arkansas, and Troy, Alabama. The expansion adds 100,000 square feet and converts 85,000 square feet of office space to factory floor, taking the plant past 735,000 square feet.

Defense News

An Australian firm will let Boxer crews direct drones by voice

Defense News reports that Sydney-based Breaker Industries is working with Rheinmetall Defence Australia on Avalon, a human-machine interface meant to let a Boxer commander direct unmanned systems in the air and on the ground. The firms signed a teaming agreement Oct. 6 at the Land Forces exhibition in Perth. Work continues for twelve months, with trials slated for mid-2027.

A dating standard worth keeping is a man who does not watch porn

Friday's First Things essay says some conservative young men call women's dating standards unrealistic, and that viral checklists about height and income give that complaint a foothold. The writer draws a line: character standards still matter, and refusing a partner who watches pornography is one of them. Some conservatives, the essay says, treat that refusal as unrealistic and dismiss it as feminist.
