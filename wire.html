U.S. and Colombian soldiers ran jungle warfare drills together at Tolemaida as Exercise Southern Vanguard opened

An Army still posted Nov. 3 shows Colombian Army Brig. Gen. Edilberto Cortes introducing himself to U.S. soldiers during jungle training at Tolemaida, Colombia, as the two armies opened Southern Vanguard 27. Defense News reported Friday that about 350 U.S. troops and more than 400 Colombian soldiers kicked off the 41st iteration of the exercise, which runs through mid-November and is hosted by the Colombian Army and led by U.S. Army Western Hemisphere Command. The drills cover jungle warfare, counter-IED work, command-post training, maritime interdiction, cyber skills, and ground security. Cortes called the training a shared commitment to collective readiness, and medical evacuation drills were planned from Tolemaida to the Cavalry School in Bogota.

Britain and France are leading 2,800 troops from 11 nations in a Poland exercise rehearsing a postwar Ukraine deployment

Defense News, Saturday: the exercise, KOALICJA 26.2, runs Nov. 9 to 22 across Poland, France, and Turkey under the Coalition of the Willing's military arm, the Multinational Force-Ukraine. American troops will not take part. A British Ministry of Defence statement says the drills combine air, land, and sea forces to test how to move, protect, and supply a force that would help rebuild Ukraine's armed forces once the fighting ends. Belgium, Estonia, Germany, Lithuania, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, and Britain are also taking part alongside France. Defense News reports U.S., Russian, and Ukrainian negotiators are set to meet in Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10 and 11.

III Marine Expeditionary Force extended a junior-ranks curfew on Okinawa after a Marine was indicted on murder charges

USNI News, Friday: III MEF extended the curfew for grades E-1 through E-4 for 30 more days, restricting them to personal housing from midnight to 5 a.m., and kept restrictions on alcohol purchase and consumption for all service members on Okinawa. The curfew had been set to expire Sunday. Japanese prosecutors indicted a Marine lance corporal on murder and robbery charges over the suspected killing of a woman on the island, and the command has not named a new end date for the restrictions.

The Army renamed its cooks tactical field feeding specialists to match a battlefield logistics mission

Defense News, Friday, citing a late-October ceremony at Fort Lee, Virginia: soldiers in the 92G occupation, formerly culinary specialists, are now tactical field feeding specialists. Quartermaster commandant Col. Stacy Moore-Callaway said the service changes an MOS title not for superficial rebranding but because the character of war has changed, and that 92G soldiers who prepare meals now also need to understand battlefield logistics in a contested environment. The Army first formalized the cook position as 94B Food Service Specialist in the 1960s and redesignated it 92G Culinary Specialist in 1995. Stars and Stripes first reported the change.

French sailors ran a close-in defense drill aboard a combat supply ship built to protect itself without an escort

USNI News, Friday, reporting from Toulon: a team of French sailors donned gloves and flash hoods aboard the Batiment ravitailleur de forces-class resupply ship FS Jacques Stosskopf for a close-in defense drill. The ship's short-range combat center and close-in weapon systems have let it operate in the Middle East, including the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, the center's commanding officer told reporters aboard the vessel. In 2023, the French Navy was among the first European fleets to provide air defense for merchant vessels transiting the contested strait under Operation Prosperity Guardian.

A Law & Liberty review says the Social Network sequel stages a reckoning that never actually happened

Law & Liberty, Friday: the reviewer, a former Facebook-funded fact-checking intern, writes that The Social Reckoning follows Facebook whistleblower Frances Haugen, Wall Street Journal reporter Jeff Horwitz, and Facebook CEO Mark Zuckerberg through the fallout of the 2021 Facebook Papers. Jeremy Strong plays a stone-faced Zuckerberg preparing for congressional testimony in a staged mock courtroom, while Mikey Madison and Jeremy Allen White play Haugen and Horwitz. The review argues the film implies real consequences landed on Facebook's leadership when, in the writer's account, no such reckoning actually took place, and that the billions of posts and every-language scale of online misinformation made the author's own fact-checking internship a pitiful solution regardless of funding.

A Providence review credits a new book's access to Netanyahu's war cabinet while faulting its rushed pace

Providence, Friday: the review of Yonah Jeremy Bob and Elliot Kaufman's In the War Room says the book is based on extensive interviews with Prime Minister Netanyahu, Defense Minister Yoav Gallant, and Cabinet Minister Benny Gantz, tracing how Israel came back from the October 7, 2023 defeat to what the authors call a stronger military-strategic position than it has had in decades. The authors trace the catastrophic deterrence failure that let the Hamas attack happen to overconfidence in surveillance technology, unready ground forces, and partisan political conflict, then follow the Gaza counterattack, the Hezbollah pager operation and leadership strikes in September 2024, the killing of Yahya Sinwar, and Operation Rising Lion against Iran's nuclear program in June 2025. The reviewer's first complaint is that the book's fast, dramatic pace skips past chances for deeper questioning of the Israeli military's own account.
