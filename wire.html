Eisenhower heads back to Norfolk after a Growler mishap injured four sailors

Aircraft carrier USS Dwight D. Eisenhower (CVN-69) is returning to Naval Station Norfolk after a Sept. 28 incident that injured four sailors and lost an EA-18G Growler, USNI News reported Saturday. The return is to allow a full look at the ship’s aircraft launch and recovery gear. USNI said the carrier will come back to Norfolk for that assessment.

Pentagon restates a Pacific denial line and the munitions it would take

USNI News reported Friday that the Pentagon restated plans for a forward area-denial network along the First Island Chain to deter China, in a 2026 defense update. In a Sept. 22 report to the Senate Armed Services Committee, a defense under secretary said U.S. forces are working on hardened command and control, logistics under fire, and magazine depth for that posture.

Heritage counts a fraud-task-force cut of more than 760,000 ACA enrollments

Heritage writes that the administration’s Task Force to Eliminate Fraud is removing more than 760,000 unverified enrollments from Affordable Care Act rolls, which the piece says yields $2.2 billion in savings. The task force was stood up earlier this year.

Heritage argues the Navy still needs escort carriers, not only the big decks

Heritage’s Friday commentary says the first question in a crisis is still where the nearest carrier is, then argues the fleet needs smaller escort carriers alongside the large decks. The piece treats hull count and presence as the gap, not a press release.

Saudi planners weigh a large Yemeni push to break the Houthi hold on the Red Sea

Defense News reported Friday that Saudi officials are planning an assault on the Houthis aimed at the Red Sea choke point. Officials said more than 100,000 Yemeni troops could be mobilized, depending on the scale.

Navy awards RTX a $24.4 billion deal for more than 1,900 SM-6 missiles

USNI News reported the Navy contracted RTX to build more than 1,900 Standard Missile-6s over five years, a $24.4 billion award announced Thursday. The Navy said the deal gives industry a demand signal to expand production and deepen the magazine.
