U.S. F-35s join an Indian air exercise starting Saturday

Defense News, Friday, from New Delhi: Air Force F-35s will fly in India's Tarang Shakti drills at Jodhpur, the first time the jet undertakes tactical missions with Indian fighters from an Indian base.
