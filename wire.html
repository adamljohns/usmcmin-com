USS Arleigh Burke enters dry dock in Spain, a first for the forward-deployed destroyers

The guided-missile destroyer USS Arleigh Burke entered dry dock at the Navantia shipyard in Cadiz, Spain, on Oct. 7. U.S. 6th Fleet says it is the first dry docking in that theater since the Forward Deployed Naval Forces-Europe destroyers arrived. Vice Adm. J.T. Anderson, the 6th Fleet commander, called it a milestone in doing planned maintenance overseas instead of sending the ship home. Crew and Forward Deployed Regional Maintenance Center personnel are working the availability alongside Navantia. Arleigh Burke is one of five destroyers stationed at Naval Station Rota and assigned to Task Force 65.

Heritage says the 22-month hold on 2024 federal election records has now run out

Thursday's commentary by Ken Cuccinelli says federal law required records tied to the November 2024 election to be kept for 22 months, and that clock has now expired. Officials who destroy those files after the deadline are inside the statute. The piece argues they should keep the evidence anyway. It is a commentary, not a new statute.

Law & Liberty asks whether Buckley wanted a catechetical college

Cheryl Miller's forum essay, posted Wednesday, answers Robert P. George on God and Man at Yale at 75. George wants a balanced curriculum and rejects catechesis in the classroom. Miller writes that Buckley wanted a formative university, not a catechetical one, and that the student was still to remain the final arbiter. She notes state boards are now standing up civics schools where private trustees have not. It is an argument, not a new statute.

Tech / AI

France fires an upgraded ballistic missile from a submarine for the first time

Defense News reported Tuesday that the nuclear-powered ballistic-missile submarine Le Vigilant fired an M51.3 on Oct. 6 in an acceptance test after the boat's overhaul. The French Ministry of the Armed Forces said the missile carried no nuclear warhead, launched off Finistère, and came down in the North Atlantic several hundred kilometers from any coast. President Emmanuel Macron watched from a French Navy vessel and called it a step for strategic credibility. The ministry said the missile entered operational service last October after a 2023 land test. Range figures in the piece come from the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, not from Paris.

Air Force is studying Army-style industry access to military test ranges

Defense News reported Thursday that Brig. Gen. Mark Massaro, commander of the Air Force Test Center, told a Mitchell Institute forum the service is working with the Army on how to open ranges to approved companies. Earlier this year the Army opened five ranges, four in the United States and one overseas. Massaro said the Air Force has talked with Army Test and Evaluation Command and that he arranged smaller tests for contractors while commanding the 96th Test Wing at Eglin. He also said overhead imagery and drone incursions are a reason to protect what is being tested. It is a study, not an open-gate order.

WORLD says Sorkin's Facebook film moves January 6 nine months to make a point

Thursday's review of Aaron Sorkin's The Social Reckoning says the film cuts a congressional hearing against the January 6 Capitol riot as if the two happened together. They were nine months apart, and the Facebook Files ran in September 2021, later than the movie implies. The reviewer also notes the film shows people radicalized against masks and vaccines and does not show left-wing radicalization. Frances Haugen, at a Washington screening, said the real reporter is not as lost as the character and that teen mental health was a larger part of her case than the film allows. It is a review, not a court finding.
