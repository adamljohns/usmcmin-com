Defense News: Navy awards Boeing first MQ-25A Stingray production contract

Defense News, Tuesday: the Navy awarded Boeing a $562 million fixed-price incentive contract Monday to start Lot 1 low-rate initial production of the MQ-25A Stingray unmanned tanker. The award covers three Lot 1 aircraft plus components for three Lot 2 airframes, with first Lot 1 deliveries slated for 2029. The program of record is 67 operational aircraft and nine test aircraft. Production start, not a carrier-embarked fleet.
