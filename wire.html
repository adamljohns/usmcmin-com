A federal ruling opened Navy drone-boat contract decisions made through Other Transactions to court review

The Court of Federal Claims rejected the government's claim that its Other Transaction competition was beyond judicial review, giving Saildrone and Blue Water Autonomy a forum to challenge the Navy's unmanned-vessel selection process. The court found disparate treatment in the review of Saildrone's proposal and ordered the Navy to reevaluate it, adding a judicial check to a contracting tool the Pentagon has increasingly favored.

Ron Wyden says restoring trade with Canada will be a top priority if he takes over the Senate Finance Committee

Wyden said he will push to reverse President Trump's tariffs on Canadian goods and is trying to build a coalition large enough to withstand a veto. He still supports targeted tariffs against unfair Chinese trade practices, but argues that tariffs should be one tool among several rather than the default response.

WORLD

The European Commission withdrew its plan to make hate speech an EU-level crime after member-state resistance

The proposal would have placed hate speech and hate crime in the EU's category of especially serious cross-border crimes alongside terrorism and human trafficking. The Commission is considering a narrower proposal focused on online incitement to violence and hatred, while Alliance Defending Freedom International warns that vague standards could still reach peaceful religious speech.

Culture

Heritage argues that the Founders tied immigration policy to cultural fit and the preservation of ordered liberty

The report says early American statesmen understood that immigration at sufficient scale can alter a nation's habits, institutions and capacity for self-government. Its argument is historical and prescriptive: immigration decisions should weigh whether newcomers can be incorporated into a regime of natural rights, religious liberty and constitutional government.

TruthScript warns Christians against letting political disappointment drive them toward online rumor and emotional manipulation

Drawing on Absalom's rebellion and Proverbs 18:17, the essay argues that fickleness and gullibility reinforce one another when people accept the first attractive claim without testing it. Its remedy is disciplined attention to Scripture, evidence and real relationships instead of an endless cycle of social-media suspicion.

Britain committed £90 million to an autonomous North Atlantic network for hunting submarines and guarding undersea cables

Atlantic Net will combine drones, sensors, communications and data services so the Royal Navy can detect hostile underwater activity earlier. The first phase is expected to begin operating within months under a framework led by Ocean Infinity Solutions, with the system designed to support NATO and northern European maritime security.
