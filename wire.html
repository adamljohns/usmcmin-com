Carrier Air Wing 9 closed a 10-month combat deployment aboard Abraham Lincoln

DVIDS reported that Carrier Air Wing 9 concluded the deployment aboard USS Abraham Lincoln on Oct. 8, after embarking Nov. 21, 2025. The release says the wing flew 14,037 sorties and 31,460 flight hours, and that Operation Epic Fury, ordered Feb. 28, ran 38 days before an early April ceasefire. It says the wing expended 1,499 precision weapons, more than 1.37 million pounds of ordnance, against U.S. Central Command targets. Capt. J.W. Stigi, the wing commander, said the force destroyed Iran's conventional Navy and secured maritime chokepoints. Destroyers Spruance and Michael Murphy remain underway in the 7th and 5th Fleets. The still shows an F-35C from Marine Fighter Attack Squadron 314 on the flight deck Oct. 1, while the ship was still in the 3rd Fleet.

Tech / AI

Lockheed unveiled a PAC-3 variant aimed at hypersonic threats

Defense News, citing Reuters, reported Saturday that Lockheed Martin unveiled PAC-3 Edge, an interceptor meant to counter hypersonic and advanced missile threats at longer ranges and higher altitudes. The company said it adds a new seeker, upgraded flight controls, and a multi-pulse rocket motor from Northrop Grumman. Tim Cahill, president of missiles and fire control, told Reuters the cost should be about that of the existing PAC-3 MSE, and that full production would run about 2,000 a year. The Army awarded Lockheed a contract worth up to $58.6 billion in July to ramp up current PAC-3 production. This is a company unveiling, not a signed order for the new variant.

The Defense Department identified 250 missing service members in fiscal 2026

A DVIDS release from Washington says the Department of War identified 250 service members in fiscal year 2026, a record, under the accounting mission that treats no fallen comrade as left behind. The release is the official count posted this week. It does not list the individual cases.
