Carrier Eisenhower heads back to Norfolk after losing a Growler in a landing mishap

USS Dwight D. Eisenhower is returning to Naval Station Norfolk after a Sept. 28 landing mishap that cost the Navy an EA-18G Growler and injured four sailors, USNI News reported Saturday. The Navy said the jet, from the Whidbey Island-based “Zappers” of Electronic Attack Squadron 130, had an arresting-gear failure while landing off the Virginia coast. Both aviators ejected and were picked up by search-and-rescue crews from Helicopter Sea Combat Squadron 7. The Navy said the return to port will allow a comprehensive assessment after the mishap. It has not said how long Ike will stay pierside or how the carrier’s training schedule will shift.

Saudi Arabia plans an offensive to retake the Red Sea strait from the Houthis

Gulf, Yemeni and Western officials told Reuters the Saudi-backed push could come within weeks, led by Yemeni ground forces with Saudi air strikes. The Houthis seized the Bab el-Mandeb strait last month. Officials said more than 100,000 Yemeni troops could be mobilized, and the U.S. is already supplying intelligence.

Fraud task force says it is dropping 760,000 unverified enrollees from ACA rolls

The administration’s Task Force to Eliminate Fraud says removing the unverified Affordable Care Act enrollees saves $2.2 billion. Heritage calls it another win for the task force; the figures are the government’s own claim.

Italy’s coalition moves to cut up to €8 billion from planned defense spending

A Cabinet decision Thursday, pushed by Deputy Prime Minister Matteo Salvini, would trim the defense money Italy expected under an EU deficit exemption from about €21 billion to around €14 billion. A government source called the cut agreed but not yet final. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni has backed Ukraine and NATO targets, but voters and coalition partners are pulling the other way.

France plans to fly fighters with AI-driven combat drones in 2028

The French Air Force, its armament directorate and the defense AI agency are working on a 2028 flight pairing crewed jets with combat drones under a project called Hypairion. Officials want government-owned, open software for command and mission autonomy.
