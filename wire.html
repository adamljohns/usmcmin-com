An F-35C made the first arrested landing on a Ford-class carrier

DVIDS posted a Navy photograph taken Oct. 13 in the Atlantic of an F-35C Lightning II, attached to the Salty Dogs of Air Test and Evaluation Squadron 23, making an arrested landing on PCU John F. Kennedy (CVN 79). The caption says it is the first time an F-35C has landed on a Ford-class aircraft carrier. Kennedy is underway for Aircraft Compatibility Testing, which checks the ship's launch and recovery gear against Navy aircraft.

A Yale argument still turns on who gets to defend the truth

Wednesday's Law & Liberty forum reply by James Hankins answers Robert P. George on William F. Buckley's God and Man at Yale. Hankins agrees that a university should not present dogmas for unreflective belief, and that requiring professors to promote some ideas and deflate others would mean monitoring incompatible with liberal education. He prefers Aristotle's claim in the Rhetoric that the true and the just are naturally easier to prove, and that a defeat of truth is usually the fault of its advocates.

Tech / AI

The Army moved 600 enlisted soldiers into a permanent space specialty

Defense News reported Wednesday that the Army selected more than 600 soldiers to reclassify as MOS 40D Tactical Space Operations Specialists, the Space Operations Branch's first permanent enlisted cadre. Command Sgt. Maj. Rickey Jackson said nearly 3,000 applied, drawn from Military Intelligence, Air Defense Artillery, and the Signal Corps. Lt. Gen. John L. Rafferty said temporary rotations back to a basic branch will not meet future demand. The branch was established in June. Training is at the Space and Missile Defense Center of Excellence in Colorado Springs.

Defense News

Army and Navy labs kept a field shelter working at 65 below

Defense News reported Wednesday that the Naval Facilities Engineering and Expeditionary Warfare Center and the Army's Cold Regions Research and Engineering Laboratory tested a rapidly deployable Triple Container rigid-wall shelter for Arctic cold. The fielded kit was rated to minus 15 degrees Fahrenheit. The test goal was minus 50. The release said the TRICON system stayed operational down to minus 65 in CRREL's New Hampshire cold chamber, which can reach minus 90.
