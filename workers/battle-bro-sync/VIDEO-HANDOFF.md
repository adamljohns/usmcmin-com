# OpenClaw / Max — Battle Brother explainer video

**Priority:** next (magic-link sign-in is working)  
**Owner:** Max  
**Form:** https://usmcmin.com/family-captain/battle-bro-form/viewform.html  
**Audience:** one Captain + his Battle Brother (not a crowd)

## Goal

A short, calm Captain-voice walkthrough of the Battle Brother module so a new brother can use it without Adam on the call.

## Tone

- Direct, plain, no hype, no exclamation-point energy
- Talk to one operator
- Show the screen; narrate what to tap

## Suggested runtime

4–7 minutes. One take is fine if clear.

## Shot list (in order)

1. **Open the form** — URL above (bookmark it).
2. **Account · Cloud Sync** — enter email → Email magic link → open the email → sign in. Note: works across phone + laptop once signed in.
3. **Habit Board** — switch to Habit Board · Share. Point out:
   - Calendar date above weekday
   - Defaults: Review Creed → 5:25 Prayer → Captain’s Log → Complete my SACA → Battle Brother call / His SACA complete → Armada call
   - Tap cells to check; weekly habits land on Sunday
   - Private vs Shared toggle
   - Add habit if he wants extras (Family meeting, Body line, etc.)
4. **Share with Battle Brother** — pair by email invite / accept code (preferred). Mention JSON pack only as offline fallback.
5. **Weekly check-in form** — walk the four steps briefly (SACA, brother call + 3 questions, Armada call + rating, confidential comments). Submit once.
6. **Close** — “Do this every week. Call your brother. Keep the board honest.”

- Optional later: embed on the form page under Account or a “How this works” link — **do not embed until Adam picks the file location**
